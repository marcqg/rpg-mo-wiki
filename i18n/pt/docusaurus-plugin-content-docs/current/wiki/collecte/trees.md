## **Trees**

Fir through Blue Palm can also be cut in the [Woodcutting Guild](/woodcutting-guild) located in [Moche I](/moche-i) close to the [Dorpat](/dorpat) Traveler's Sign entrance by crossing three bridges to your left. [Woodcutting Guild Permission](/woodcutting-guild-permission) can be bought from the [Cesis Lumberjack](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Cesis_Lumberjack) for 98k coins.

NOTE: Each woodcutting level adds a 1% chance to successfully cutting each log.
