Soil [![Soil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/42/Soil.png/revision/latest?cb=20240210202541)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/42/Soil.png/revision/latest?cb=20240210202541) is crafted using the [Carpentry](/carpentry) skill at level 0 with Vials Of Water (35 [Small](/small-vial-of-water), 25 [Medium](/medium-vial-of-water), 15 [Large](/large-vial-of-water) **or** 15 [Superior](/superior-vial-of-water)) at your [Player Island](/player-island), and is used to grow things with the [Farming](/farming) skill.
