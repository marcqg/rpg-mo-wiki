**Steel Scimitar** is a level 40 one-handed weapon. It can be crafted by a player with 56 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 6 [Steel Bars](/steel-bar) and 3 [Willow Logs](/willow-log). The chance of successfully crafting one is 70% and rewards 240 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Steel Scimitar can be enchanted into an Enchanted Steel Scimitar at an Anvil.
