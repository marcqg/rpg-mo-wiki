## Craftable

Smelt 2 Sand [![Sand-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/50/Sand-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211251)](/sand) at a [Furnace](/furnace) [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Alchemy](/alchemy) level 65, Min chance 20% - Max 30% - 30 exp.

Combine 3 [Empty Large Vials](/empty-large-vial)[![Empty Large Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/af/Empty_Large_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155259)](/empty-large-vial) at a [Furnace](/furnace)[![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Forging](/forging) level 50, 100% chance - 6 exp.

Fill with water from a town [Water Well](/water-well)[![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601) to create a [Superior Vial of Water](/superior-vial-of-water)[![Superior Vial Of Water](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/b/bd/Superior_Vial_Of_Water.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215330)](/superior-vial-of-water)
