**Fire Bolt** is a low level [Magic](/magic) spell that has a maximum damage of 5 and has 75 casts per scroll. Each cast grants 9 Magic [experience](/experience) regardless of damage dealt. Fire Bolt scrolls can be crafted by using a Fire Bolt Medallion and a [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) at a Fire Altar. A player with level 19 [Wizardry](/wizardry) has a 70% chance of successfully crafting the scroll; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Wizardry level. Successfully crafting the scroll grants 15 Wizardry experience.
