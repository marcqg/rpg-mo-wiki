**Beast Herb Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 69 [Farming](/farming) and produces a Beast Herb after 34 minutes. Harvesting the plant grants 145 Farming [experience](/experience).
