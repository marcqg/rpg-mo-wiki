**Iron Scythe** is a two-handed weapon that can be equipped at 20 [Strength](/strength). It can be crafted by a player with level 32 [Forging](/forging) by using 6 [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) and 3 [Oak Logs](/oak-log) on an [Anvil](/anvil) with a success rate of 85%. Successfully crafting the scythe grants 144 Forging [experience](/experience).

It can be enchanted into an [Enchanted Iron Scythe](/enchanted-iron-scythe).
