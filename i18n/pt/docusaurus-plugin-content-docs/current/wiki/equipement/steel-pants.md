**Steel Pants** are pants that require 40 [Defense](/defense) to equip. A player with 52 [Forging](/forging) has a 70% chance of crafting a pair of pants with 6 [steel bars](/steel-bar) at an [anvil](/anvil) while having a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped. Successfully crafting a pair of pants will grant 210 Forging [experience](/experience).
