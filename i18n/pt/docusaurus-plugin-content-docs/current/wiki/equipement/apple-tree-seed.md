**Apple Tree Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 8 [Farming](/farming) and produces an [Apple](/apple) after 8 minutes. Harvesting the apple grants 16 Farming [experience](/experience).
