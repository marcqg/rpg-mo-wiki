**Bamboo Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 20 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Bamboo Fabric](/bamboo-fabric) after 13 minutes. Harvesting the fabric grants 16 Farming [experience](/experience).
