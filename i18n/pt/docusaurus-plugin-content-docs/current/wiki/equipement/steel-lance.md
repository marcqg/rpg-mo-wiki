**Steel Lance** is a level 40 one-handed weapon. It can be obtained by a player with level 58 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 7 [Steel Bars](/steel-bar) and 4 [Willow Logs](/willow-log). The chance of successfully crafting one is 70% and rewards 285 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Steel Lance can be enchanted into an Enchanted Steel Lance.
