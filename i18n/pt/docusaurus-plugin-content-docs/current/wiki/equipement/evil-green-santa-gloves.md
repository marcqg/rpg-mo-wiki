**Evil Green Santa Gloves** is a [2021 Christmas event](https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=11254) item. It can be obtained by almost every Mob in the game at various low rates. These gloves are a part of the [Evil Green Santa Costume Box](/evil-green-santa-costume-box) set.
