**Raw Trout** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), and can be cooked into a [Trout](/trout). Raw Trout cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)
*   Fishing Off Dorpat Coast.

### Drops

*   [Skeleton Fighter](/skeleton-fighter) (5%)
