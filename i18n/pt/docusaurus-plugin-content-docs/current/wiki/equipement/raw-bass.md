1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw FishA **Raw Bass** is a type of fish that can be caught at [Fishing](/fishing) level 40 with a [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) giving 30 fishing experience. It can be cooked into a [Bass](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Bass) at [Cooking](/cooking) level 40 giving 57 cooking experience. When cooked and eaten, Bass heals 10 [Health](/health).

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Thief](/thief) (5%)
