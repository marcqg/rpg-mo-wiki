**Cotton Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 3 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) after 5 minutes. Harvesting the cotton grants 10 Farming [experience](/experience).
