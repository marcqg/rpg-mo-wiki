**Steel Long Sword** is a level 40 [Accuracy](/accuracy) weapon. It can be crafted by a player with level 48 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 4 [Steel Bars](/steel-bar) and 2 [Willow Logs](/willow-log). The chance of successfully crafting one is 70% and rewards 160 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Steel Long Sword can be enchanted into an Enchanted Steel Long Sword.
