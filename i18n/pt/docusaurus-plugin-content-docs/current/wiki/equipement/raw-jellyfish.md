1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw Fish**Raw Jellyfish** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Net](/fishing-net), and can be cooked into a [Jellyfish](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Jellyfish). The Raw Jellyfish cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Sand Golem](/sand-golem) (3%)
