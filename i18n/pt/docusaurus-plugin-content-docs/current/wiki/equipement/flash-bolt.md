Flash Bolt ![Flash Bolt](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/09/Flash_Bolt.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225231821) is a [spell](/spells) you can use at level 1 [magic](/magic) which adds 2 magic damage. It can be bought from the [Dorpat Magician](/dorpat-magician) for 375.

## Crafting

The Flash Bolt spell is made from [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) and a Flash Bolt Medallion (not consumed) at the Flash Altar in [Reval](/reval).

## [Monster](/monsters) Drops

*   [Shadow Ghost](/shadow-ghost)
*   [Gray Wizard](/gray-wizard)
*   [Draman](/draman)
