Nephilim Warrior is quest mob # 61, [Got Wings?](/quests), with possible rewards of 33,900 exp, 10 MOS or 94,860 coins. He is located in Heaven and [Every Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Every_Man%27s_Land) if you use a [Secret Boss Scroll](/secret-boss-scroll).
