The Skeleton is an aggressive CL 36 NPC monster located in the [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) and [Walco](/walco).

## Locations

| Location | Quantity | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Walco](/walco) | 8 | (77,39) |
| [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) | 10 | (75,29) |

[Walco](/walco): Heading south from the [Illusion Gate](/illusion-gate) in the NE corner of the map should eventually bring one to the graveyard containing Skeletons.

[Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon): Find the ladder (Point D, on the World Map) that is southwest of the Dorpat Outpost. The Dorpat Outpost is in the southeastern region of [Dorpat](/dorpat).
