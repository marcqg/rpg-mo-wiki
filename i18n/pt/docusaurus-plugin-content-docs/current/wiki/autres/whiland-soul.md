**Whiland Soul** is a soul item that is used to make teleports to [Whiland](/whiland). It can be obtained as a mob drop.

## Alchemy

A Memory of Whiland can be crafted with a [Large Vial Of Water](/large-vial-of-water) and four Whiland Souls. A player with level 55 [Alchemy](/alchemy) has a success rate of 40%; the success rate can be raised to a maximum of 60% with an increased Alchemy level. Successfully crafting the potion will grant 82 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

## Wizardry

A Whiland Teleport can be crafted at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped and four [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) and four Whiland Souls. A player needs level 55 [Wizardry](/wizardry) and there is a flat success rate of 60%. Successfully crafting the scroll will grant 52 Wizardry experience.
