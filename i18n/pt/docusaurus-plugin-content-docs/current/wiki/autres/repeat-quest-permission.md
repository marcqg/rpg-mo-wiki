The **Repeat Quest Permission** allows a player to redo a [Quest](/quests) of their choice.

*   If you use this item, it will be deleted and the selected quest will be displayed in the active directory again
*   It must be in your [Inventory](/inventory) to use. You can also use it to lower the [Party Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) and [Cathedral](/cathedral) cool down timers by 1 hour each

Most Mobs can drop these when killed, and new players will receive one when leaving the [Tutorial Island](/tutorial-island).

Note: Completing a quest gives experience, MOS or coins randomly. Repeating a quest will give a NEW random bonus.
