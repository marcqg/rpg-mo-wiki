Back item, obtained by enchanting [Firelord Cape +2](/firelord-cape). Can be enchanted up to +5, at +6 turns into [Blaze Dragon Wings +6](/blaze-dragon-wings).

## Dragon Wings +3

Requires 30 Defense to wear, offfers 45 armor.

## Dragon Wings +4

Requires 40 Defense to wear, offfers 55 armor.

## Dragon Wings +5

Requires 50 Defense to wear, offfers 70 armor.
