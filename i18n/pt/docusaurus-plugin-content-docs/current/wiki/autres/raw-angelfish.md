1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw FishA **Raw Angelfish** is a type of fish that can be caught at [Fishing](/fishing) level 45 with a [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) giving 40 fishing experience. It can be cooked into an [Angelfish](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Angelfish) at [Cooking](/cooking) level 45 giving 72 cooking experience. When cooked and eaten, Angelfish heals 11 [Health](/health).
