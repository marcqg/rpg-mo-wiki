The Ice Tower is located in [Void I](/void-i) land. It is the medium difficulty of the three towers, [Nature Tower](/nature-tower) being the easiest and [Fire Tower](/fire-tower) being the hardest. Completing each room gains you from 750 to 2000 experience points towards your [Fighting Mode](/fighting-mode) stance. If you use arrows to kill the mob, [Archery](/archery) skill gets the bonus. There are 65 (Elite) mob rooms and [Deadly Tree](/deadly-tree) is in room 66. Like [Cathedral](/cathedral), all of your items are safe when you die and you exit outside of Ice Tower with full health.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding the [Deadly Tree](/deadly-tree) is 427,750. Without using [Magic](/magic) and assuming a [combat stance](/fighting-mode) other than [Cordial](/fighting-mode), finishing the Ice Tower will grant you a total of 1,415,625 combat experience with 943,750 for your current [combat stance](/fighting-mode) and 471,875 for [Health](/health). This includes the total bonus experience of 88,250 which comes from dealing the final hit to each mob. Using the [Cordial](/fighting-mode) combat stance will give 2/3 of the total experience normally gained with all of the experience going to [Health](/health). Using [Magic](/magic) will change the mob experience due to how [Magic](/magic) experience is calculated.

The bonus experience per room is 750 for rooms 1 through 19; 1,500 for rooms 20 through 55; and 2,000 for rooms 56 through 65.
