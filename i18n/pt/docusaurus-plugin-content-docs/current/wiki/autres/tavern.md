The Tavern is a meeting place accessed from most towns. There are four special chests there for using your Boss Retaliation keys or a Rare key. Rare mobs are listed [Here](/rare-mob-spawns). The Tavern Shopkeeper sells Lemonade, Eggplant Seeds & Lottery Tickets. Tavern has its own chat channel called \[LC\]. Use up/down arrow keys to change to this channel when in Tavern area.

Locations are: [Dorpat](/dorpat), [Walco](/walco), [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest), [Whiland](/whiland), [Rakblood](/rakblood), [Narwa](/narwa), [Fellin Island](/fellin-island), [Reval](/reval), [Cesis](/cesis) and Witches II.
