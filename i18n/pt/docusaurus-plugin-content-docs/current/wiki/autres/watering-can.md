Watering cans are used to revive wilted crops. Wilting takes one week in real time after crops are ripe. They are crafted at the [Anvil](/forging) [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)and refilled at a town [Water Well](/water-well) [![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](/water-well)Better watering cans hold more water for less refilling and provide one exp per use which reduces grow time by one minute.
