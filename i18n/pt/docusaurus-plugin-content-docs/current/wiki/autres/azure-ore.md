## Use

At lvl 65 Forging, you can combine 4x **Azure Ores** at a Furnace to make [Azurite Bar](/azurite-bar).

Can be sold on the player market for more coins than to NPC.

## Mining

Requires lvl 60 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool. Recommended mining level is 80+.

Mined from [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle), [Blood River](/blood-river), [Hell](/hell), [Lost Woods](/lost-woods), and No Man's Dungeon.

Recommended for lower level players are Lost Woods (bank in Whiland, here is a helpful map: [https://motavern.com/maps/lw](https://motavern.com/maps/lw)) and No-man's dungeon (pvp zone. The mine itself is in a safe zone but you can be killed en route to the chest, so only bring your pickaxe, pet and boots). At cl 135+, the mine in Hell becomes good, as all mobs on the way have become passive by then and you only have to kill one archdevil.
