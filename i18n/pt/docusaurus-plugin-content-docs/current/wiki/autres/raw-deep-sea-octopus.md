**Raw Deep Sea Octopus** is an uncooked food item. It can be caught at a level 65-80 Fishing Rod Spot or a level 65-80 Iron Fishing Rod Spot by a player with level 80 [Fishing](/fishing). These fishing spots can be found in [Fellin Island](/fellin-island), the Illusion Guild, and [Whiland](/whiland). Catching a deep sea octopus rewards 100 Fishing [experience](/experience).

A player with level 80 [Cooking](/cooking) can cook it into a [Cooked Deep Sea Octopus](/cooked-deep-sea-octopus) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) with an initial success rate of 30%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping at level 150). Successfully cooking a spiny sea star rewards 200 Cooking experience.
