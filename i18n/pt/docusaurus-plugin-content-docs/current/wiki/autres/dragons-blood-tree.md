[![Dragon's Blood Tree](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e2/Dragon%27s_Blood_Tree.png/revision/latest?cb=20170622214149)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e2/Dragon%27s_Blood_Tree.png/revision/latest?cb=20170622214149) The Dragon's Blood Tree can be cut at level 105 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) using a Steel Woodcutters Axe or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool). When cut successfully, a [Dragon's Blood Log](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Blood_Log) is acquired. The log can be used in skills such as [Carpentry](/carpentry) and [Fletching](/fletching).
