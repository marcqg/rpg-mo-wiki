## Mining

Kaolinite Clay can be mined at level 50 mining with an [Iron Pickaxe](/iron-pickaxe), [Steel Pickaxe](/steel-pickaxe) or [Multi-Tool](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-Tool).
