A Potion of Oblivion [![Potion Of Oblivion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/eb/Potion_Of_Oblivion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226014435)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/eb/Potion_Of_Oblivion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226014435)is used to remove one skill level from your choice of any skill. Especially useful for players wanting to keep their character Pure or to reduce [Combat Level](/combat-level) to make [Party Quests](/quests) easier.

You can obtain potions from [MOS Market](/mos-market), [Player Market](/market-guide) in your [Chest](/chest) or as an [Achievement Reward](/achievements).
