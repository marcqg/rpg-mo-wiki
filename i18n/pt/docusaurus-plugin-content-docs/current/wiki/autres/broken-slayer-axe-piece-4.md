**Broken Slayer Axe Piece 4** is a crafting material that can be used to make a Dragon Slayer Axe. It can be combined with a [Broken Slayer Axe Piece 1](/broken-slayer-axe-piece-1), a [Broken Slayer Axe Piece 2](/broken-slayer-axe-piece-2), and a [Broken Slayer Axe Piece 3](/broken-slayer-axe-piece-3) by a player with level 1 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool). The chance of successfully crafting a Dragon Slayer Axe is 30% and rewards 140 Forging [experience](/experience).

It can also be obtained via a [Best Present](/best-present).
