Skills are the game, some people call them stats, from combat to crafting, almost all actions are tied to a skill _(with the exception of [Enchanting](/enchanting))_. You gain [experience](/experience) to [Level](/experience-chart) your skills, by repeating everything, allowing you to wear better gear or craft and gather better items and resources, increasing the success of your skill you're [leveling](/experience-chart).

## Combat

[![2019-11-05 17-03-07](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/83/2019-11-05_17-03-07.gif/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/220?cb=20191105090524)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/83/2019-11-05_17-03-07.gif/revision/latest?cb=20191105090524)

Combat skills are used during fighting and raise your [Combat Level](/combat-level).

*   **[Accuracy](/accuracy)** - Gained by doing Melee combat damage while using Controlled or Accurate [Fighting Modes](/fighting-mode). Needed to equip 1 handed melee weapons. Determines [Hit Chance](/hit-chance) alongside Defense
*   **[Archery](/archery)** - Gained by using Bow and Arrows. Also from [Tower](/tower) room bonus if arrow is the kill shot of the mob in a room.
*   **[Defense](/defense)** - Gained by doing Melee combat damage while using Controlled or Defensive [Fighting Modes](/fighting-mode). Determines [Hit Chance](/hit-chance) alongside Accuracy
*   **[Health](/health)** - Gained by doing Melee combat damage. All [Fighting Modes](/fighting-mode) add to Health.
*   **[Magic](/magic)** - Gained by casting magic spells and using Teleport Scrolls or Books. Damage done is irrelevant.
*   **[Strength](/strength)** - Gained by doing Melee combat damage while using Controlled or Aggressive [Fighting Modes](/fighting-mode). Determines your maximum damage at 5 levels per 1 max damage done.

## Gathering

Harvesting or Gathering skills are skills used to gain resources that can be used for other skills or sold to other players.

*   **[Farming](/farming)** - Gained when you harvest a plant or pick berries.
*   **[Fishing](/fishing)** - Gained when you catch anything at a fishing spot using [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), [Fishing Net](/fishing-net), [Cage](/cage), [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon), [Steel Harpoon](/steel-harpoon), [Iron Fishing Rod](/iron-fishing-rod), [Poseidon's Trident](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Trident) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool). Each tool has its own locations. Multi-tool works in all locations if you have the proper skill level.
*   **[Mining](/mining)** - Gained when you dig sand or mine ores or veins using [Spade](/spade), [Iron Pickaxe](/iron-pickaxe), [Steel Pickaxe](/steel-pickaxe) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool).
*   **[Woodcutting](/woodcutting)** -Gained when you collect any log from any tree using [Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe), [Steel Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool).
*   **[Fungiculture](/fungiculture)** - Gained when you harvest a Fungus.

## Crafting

Crafting skills use raw materials to create a variety of types of objects in game, from consumables to equips and more.

*   **[Alchemy](/alchemy)** - Gained when you smelt sand into vials, fill vials with water, use Mortar & Pestle to grind bones/horns, craft feathers with flint, or when you craft potions. Potions craft in your inventory with Water Vial and other ingredients.
*   **[Breeding](/breeding)** - Gained when you are successful in creating an offspring. Breeding is done on your [Island](/player-island).
*   **[Carpentry](/carpentry)** - Gained when you craft Walls, Floors and other Furniture using Island Build Menu on your [Island](/player-island). Logs and other materials need to be in your [Chest](/chest) to craft.
*   **[Cooking](/cooking)** - Gained when you successfully cook a fish at the Campfire or Kettle.
*   **[Fletching](/fletching)** - Gained when you craft any Archery items. Arrows require a [Fletching Table](/fletching-table). All other gear is crafted at the [Anvil](/anvil). Failure to craft at anvil still gives you half exp.
*   **[Forging](/forging)** - Gained when crafting melee gear at the [Anvil](/anvil). Failure to craft at anvil still gives you half exp. Enchanting items gives exp also.
*   **[Jewelry](/jewelry)** - Gained when crafting jewelry items at the Furnace.
*   **[Wizardry](/wizardry)** - Gained when crafting Mage gear at the [Anvil](/anvil) or making spell scrolls at Altars.

## Equipment Bonus

Equipment bonus is an adjustment to your base fighting skills and is provided by your equipment and pet used.

*   **Armour** - 3 Armour is equal to 1 Defense.
*   **Aim** - 2 Aim is equal to 1 Accuracy.
*   **Archery** - 2 Archery (from item) is equal to 1 archery (from skill).
*   **Magic** - Accuracy of spells
*   **Power** - 10 power is equal to 5 Strength and adds +1 to max damage for melee fighting.
*   **Speed** - Movement speed to a maximum of 60 over base speed. Increase using Boots, some Wings, Archery Capes, Pets and Archery bows. Some other Archery gear and Piglet pets slow you down.

## Maximum Levels

Skills do not have a maximum level, however the observed soft cap is level 150 because it takes a long time to achieve this goal and skills do not currently have content released for beyond 150.

Temporary effects that increase or decrease skill levels are negated at 1 point per minute. Potions that increase skill levels and damage to health are the primary examples of this.
