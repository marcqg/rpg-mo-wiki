Medium magic pouch has 2 spell slots available. Each slot has a max spell count of 2,000 spells. Cannot be purchased from any NPC. Must be obtained via player market, a great present, a best present, MOS store, or from various mobs. Can also be crafted at [Wizardry](/wizardry) level 25 by combining 10 small pouches together (20% success rate, 50 exp on success)
