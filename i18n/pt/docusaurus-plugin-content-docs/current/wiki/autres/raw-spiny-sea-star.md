**Raw Spiny Sea Star** is an uncooked food item. It can be caught at a level 70-85 Fishing Net Spot by a player with level 82 [Fishing](/fishing). These fishing spots can be found in [Ancient Dungeon](/ancient-dungeon), [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest), and the Illusion Guild. Catching a spiny sea star rewards 120 Fishing [experience](/experience).

A player with level 82 [Cooking](/cooking) can cook it into a [Cooked Spiny Sea Star](/cooked-spiny-sea-star) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) with an initial success rate of 30%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping at level 152). Successfully cooking a spiny sea star rewards 240 Cooking experience.
