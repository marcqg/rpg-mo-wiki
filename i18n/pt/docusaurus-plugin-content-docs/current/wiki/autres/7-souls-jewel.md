**7 Souls Jewel** is a level 99 gemstone. It can be obtained as a drop from a mob or crafted by a player with level 99 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) using a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 2 [Cut Diamonds](/cut-diamond), 2 [Cut Rubies](/cut-ruby), 2 [Cut Emeralds](/cut-emerald), 2 [Cut Sapphires](/cut-sapphire), a Cut Amethyst, a Cut Black Opal, and a Cut Jadeite. The chance of successfully crafting one is 20% and rewards 754 Forging [experience](/experience).
