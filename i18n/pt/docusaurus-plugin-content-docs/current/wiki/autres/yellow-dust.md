**Yellow Dust** can be obtain from opening a [Good Present](/good-present) or as a mob drop.

## Recipes

### Alchemy

4x Yellow Dust and a [Superior Vial Of Water](/superior-vial-of-water) can be made into a [Potion of Preservation](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_of_Preservation).

### Breeding

Yellow Dust may also be used to [feed](/breeding-foods) the Sacred Archangel and Saint Archangel pets.
