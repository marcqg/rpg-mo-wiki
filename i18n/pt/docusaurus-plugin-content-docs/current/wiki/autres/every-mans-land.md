Every Man's Land (EML) is a hidden pvp map which is referred to in the chat tips as the Secret Cow Level.

This map is very unique due because it is marked as a "safe death" area, where players keep their items upon dying in pvp or pve.

## Accessing EML

EML is only available during special events such as Halloween and Christmas. Upon logging in, one of the messages in the chat will state that there is a Secret Cow Level,  indicating that EML is available.

To enter, speak to the Cow (NPC) located outside Dorpat town. It will create a prompt with several animal noises. Clicking "Yes" when it says "M00?" will teleport the player inside.

## Mobs

The primary population of EML are [Cow](/cow) and [Cow King.](/cow-king) EML is also home to three bosses: [\[BOSS\] Cannibal Plant,](/boss-cannibal-plant) [\[BOSS\] Nephilim Warrior, and](/boss-nephilim-warrior) [\[BOSS\] Ancient Hydra.](/boss-ancient-hydra) These bosses are locked in cages and can only be accessed using a [Secret Boss Scroll](/secret-boss-scroll) dropped by [Cow King](/cow-king) mobs nearby. Be advised that all of these bosses are accessible in other maps. EML also contains a rare [Cow](/cow) that drops a 10 hp key
