*   [![Fishing Rod](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)](/fishing-rod) Fishing Rod 5%
*   [![Iron Pickaxe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a2/Iron_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203458)](/iron-pickaxe) Iron Pickaxe 4.75%
*   [![Woodcutter's Axe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/db/Woodcutter%27s_Axe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203421)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe) Woodcutter's Axe 4.51%
*   [![Spade](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8e/Spade.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215150)](/spade) Spade 4.29%
*   [![Chisel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Chisel.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215614)](/chisel) Chisel 4.07%
*   [![Forging Hammer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a1/Forging_Hammer.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224204643)](/forging-hammer) Forging Hammer 3.87%
*   [![No loot](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/fd/No_loot.png/revision/latest?cb=20181225051736)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/fd/No_loot.png/revision/latest?cb=20181225051736) No Loot 73.51%

Tools Box can be found under Dorpat town where [Tin](/tin), [Clay](/clay) and [Copper](/copper) are found and also at the Dorpat Outpost.
