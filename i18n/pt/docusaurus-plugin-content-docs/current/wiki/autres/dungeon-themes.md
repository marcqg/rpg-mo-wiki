You can buy themes for your player dungeon for 150 [MOS](/mos) or 1.5 million [coins](/coins). Once a particular theme is bought you can change to it without further purchase, this includes the default Cave theme that is automatically available. To change to or buy a theme, be in your dungeon and select Island from the Build Menu.

Image collection of some themes used with Rapota's dungeon: [https://imgur.com/a/mVUS31d](https://imgur.com/a/mVUS31d)
