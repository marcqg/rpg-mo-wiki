**Ruby Dust** is an [Alchemy](/alchemy) item obtained by grinding [Ruby Horn](/ruby-horn) with a [Mortar And Pestle](/mortar-and-pestle) which grants 65 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

It can be used to make Call Of Angels, Call Of Glory, Call Of Lord, Call Of Ogre, Call Of Plants, Call Of Gods, Potion of Catacombs, Potion of Chaos, or Potion of Life.
