The Woodcutting Instructor can be found in [Dorpat](/dorpat) (26,27) as well as in the [Woodcutting Guild](/woodcutting-guild) located in [Moche](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Moche) (57,72). To access the Woodcutting Guild, a player must have 80 in woodcutting and have a [Woodcutting Guild Permission](/woodcutting-guild-permission) (costing 98,000) in the players inventory.

When spoken too, the instructor will recite the below text.

> "With woodcutting you can cut  
> trees and get different woods  
> that can be used to create items."

> "To cut a tree you need to equip a  
> woodcutter's axe first and then  
> click on the Fir Tree."
