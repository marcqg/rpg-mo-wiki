The _Dungeon IV_ is accessible from [Dungeon III](/dungeon-iii). There are several campfires along the dungeon and a little outpost in the East with a chest, an anvil, a furnace, a campfire and a well.

Check also all the [Locations](/locations).

  

## Entrances

From _Dungeon IV_, there are 3 entrances to _Dungeon III_. The first, which is the main entrance is in the North-West. At the South near the inner circle, there is another entrance which lead you to the citadel of the _Dungeon III_. The third entrance is at center of the map, the base of the [Titan Minotaur](/titan-minotaur) which will lead you to the lava-nest of the Blood Eagle in [Dungeon III](/dungeon-iii).
