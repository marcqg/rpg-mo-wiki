A Water Bolt Medallion [![Water Bolt Medallion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c2/Water_Bolt_Medallion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225235014)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c2/Water_Bolt_Medallion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225235014) is used either in [Spell Crafting](/spell-crafting) to make [Water Bolt](/water-bolt) or in [Forging](/forging) to make a Blizzard Medallion.

## Crafting

### Water Bolt

[Water Bolt](/water-bolt) is made from [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) with level 17 [Magic](/magic) at the Water Altar.

### Blizzard Medallion

A Blizzard Medallion requires level 80 [Forging](/forging) and is 100% successful, but gives no [Experience](/experience).

Materials:

*   **Water Bolt Medallion**
*   Water Missile Medallion
*   Water Wave Medallion
*   Water Blast Medallion
