Jail is a 1x1 area in [Dorpat](/dorpat), at coordinates (0, 0). Only Chat Moderators and Admins can decide to free a player from jail. There, a player cannot move or teleport out. Any teleport scroll used will be consumed, but its effect will be cancelled.
