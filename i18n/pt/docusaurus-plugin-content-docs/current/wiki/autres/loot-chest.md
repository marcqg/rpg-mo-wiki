(Image not available) Loot Chests were introduced with the release of [Archery](/archery) allowing distance killing of mobs and the retrieval of dropped loot. These also drop if your inventory is full allowing you to either delete an inventory item by Right Clicking (or Long Click) an item and destroying it or Right Click/Long Click on the Loot Chest and select Destroy to remove it from your path.
