Dorpat's Fishing Instructor

The fishing instructor is one of the many [NPC](/npcs) you will probably meet on your journey. He is located on the [tutorial island](/tutorial-island) as well as [Dorpat's](/dorpat) Dock.

## Dialogue

> _"I'm here to teach you about [Fishing](/fishing)."_

> _"First you need to equip a **[Fishing rod](/fishing-rod)** by clicking on it inside inventory."_

> _"After you have equipped the **Fishing rod** you have to stand on the beach line and click on the water."_

> _"You can only fish in places where a yellow text - Fish is visible. To fish with a fishing rod, find a tile named Fish - Fishing Rod."_

> _"Talk to [Cooking Instructor](/cooking-instructor) to find out how to cook the fish."_

Disclaimer: This is word for word from the game, anything that sounds weird or is grammatically incorrect is purely the game.
