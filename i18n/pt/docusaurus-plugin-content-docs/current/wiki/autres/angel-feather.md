## Dropped by

Angel Feathers are dropped by [Archangel](/archangel), [Death Angel](/death-angel), [Hell Angel](/hell-angel), and [Thunder Angel](/thunder-angel).

## Uses

Angel Feathers are used in [Forging](/forging) to craft [Angel Wings](/angel-wings), in [Alchemy](/alchemy) to craft Medium Potion of Berserker, or in [Fletching](/fletching) to create various arrows
