## Use

At lvl 90 Jewelry you can combine 6x **Redhodium Chunks** at a [Furnace](/furnace) to make Redhodium Bar.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 90 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle),[No Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/No_Man%27s_Land),Illusion Guild.
