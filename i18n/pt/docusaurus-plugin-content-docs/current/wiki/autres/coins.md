**Coins** are the most common form of currency in [RPG MO](http://www.rpg.mo.ee/). _Coins_ can be used in the [player market](/market-guide) to buy [Armors](/armors), [Foods](/foods), [Jewelry](/jewelry), [Materials](/materials), [Tools](/tools), [Weapons](/weapons), [Spells](/spells), [Pets](/pets), and [House](/carpentry) items.

## Terminology

Besides the term _coins_, players may refer to it as _gold_, _gold pieces_, _gp_, _cash_, or _money_.
