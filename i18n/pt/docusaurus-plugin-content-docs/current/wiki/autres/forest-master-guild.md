The Forest Master Guild, aka Fletching Guild, is located in Witches I by using a Forest Master Guild Permission at a ladder located in Witches II and having [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) skill of 80 or more AND [Fletching](/fletching) skill of 50 or more. The Guild Permission can be bought from the Witches II Lumberjack for 136,000 coins or from a [Great](/great-present) [![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)or [Best](/best-present) [![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)Present.
