Selecting 'Island' from the Build menu on your island lets you access Island Themes to change the look of your landscape. Each one costs 1 million coins or 100 MOS and can be changed whenever you want to. [Ship](/ship-building), furniture, farm and cabinets are not included in any theme.

There are also themes for player's dungeon [Dungeon Themes](/dungeon-themes)
