**Perch** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Perch](/raw-perch) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 0 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so, and there is a 50% chance that the Raw Perch will cook successfully.

Template:Infobox Item

## Can be Obtained From

*   Cooking [Raw Perch](/raw-perch)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Apeman](/apeman) (0.49%)
