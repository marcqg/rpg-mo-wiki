**Cooked Spiny Sea Star** is a type of food that is obtained by cooking [Raw Spiny Sea Star](/raw-spiny-sea-star) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire). A player with 82 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 30% chance of successfully cooking the spiny sea star; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping at level 152). Successfully cooking the spiny sea star grants 240 Cooking [experience](/experience). They heal 20 [Health](/health) when eaten.
