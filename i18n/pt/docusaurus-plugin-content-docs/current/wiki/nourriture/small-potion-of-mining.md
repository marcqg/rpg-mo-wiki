A **Small Potion Of Mining** is a potion made from a [Small Vial Of Water](/small-vial-of-water) and two [Skulls](/skull). A player with 7 [Alchemy](/alchemy) has a 50% chance to successfully craft the potion; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 70% with an increased Alchemy level. Successfully crafting this potion will grant 22 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

There is a 5% chance that a Small Potion of Mining (Rare) is made instead of this potion.
