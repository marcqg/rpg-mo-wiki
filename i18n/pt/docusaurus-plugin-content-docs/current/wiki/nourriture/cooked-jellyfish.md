**Cooked Jellyfish** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Jellyfish](/raw-jellyfish) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 25 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

## Can be Obtained From

*   Cooking [Raw Jellyfish](/raw-jellyfish)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Beholder](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Beholder) (5%)
*   [Dark Orc](/dark-orc) (2%)
