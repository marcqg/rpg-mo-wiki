**Cooked Baby Squid** is a type of food that is obtained by cooking [Raw Baby Squid](/raw-baby-squid) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire). A player with 5 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 50% chance of successfully cooking the squid; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level. Successfully cooking the squid grants 15 Cooking [experience](/experience). These heal 4 [Health](/health) when eaten.
