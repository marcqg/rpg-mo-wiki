**Common Starfish** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Common Starfish](/raw-common-starfish) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 44 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

*   Cooking [Raw Common Starfish](/raw-common-starfish)
