**King Emerald Dragon (Common)** is a pet that requires level 22 [Health](/health). This pet is obtained by gaining 500,000 [experience](/experience) while having an [Emerald Dragon (Common)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Emerald_Dragon_(Common)) equipped and can evolve into [Cursed Dragon (Rare)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Dragon_(Rare)) by using 2 [Stones Of Evolution](/stone-of-evolution).
