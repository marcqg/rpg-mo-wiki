The Moche Alchemist buys and sells various [alchemy](/alchemy) items. He is located at the City of Moche with coordinates of (44,57). [Moche](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Moche) can be accessed by using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in the most north-west corner of [Dorpat](/dorpat), or by using the Travelers Sign in south-west Reval.
