Allows players to enter the Mining guild in [Dorpat](/dorpat). The enterance is found surrounded by brownish rocks.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Cesis Blacksmith](/cesis-blacksmith)
*   Hell Blacksmith
*   [Great Present](/great-present)
*   [Best Present](/best-present)

## Location

[Dungeon I](/dungeon-i)

## Ores available

[Iron](/iron), [Coal](/coal), [Copper](/copper) and [Tin](/tin)

## NPC Vendor

*   [Cesis Blacksmith](/cesis-blacksmith)
