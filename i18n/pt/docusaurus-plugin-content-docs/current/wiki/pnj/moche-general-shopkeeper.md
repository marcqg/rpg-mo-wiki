The _Moche General Shopkeeper_ buys all items at 10% of the /wiki listed price. She can be found in the City of Moche with coordinates of (57,72). [Moche](/moche-i) can be accessed by using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in the north-west side of [Dorpat](/dorpat) at the [Gray Wizards](/gray-wizard), or by using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in the south-east of [Reval](/reval) west of the [Dorpat](/dorpat) [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign).
