Available from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) for 350 MOS or from your Achievement Rewards for 100 points, this item [![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728) contains a random item with a value up to 450,000 coins. Equip it in your [Inventory](/inventory) to receive your gift.

Four [![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](http://rpg-mo.wikia.com/wiki/File:Great_Present.png) [Great Presents](/great-present) can be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to make a [![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](http://rpg-mo.wikia.com/wiki/File:Best_Present.png) Best Present.
