Spell scrolls can be crafted using Medallions and Fabric. The ritual must take place at the associated Altar for the spell crafted and only the fabric is used up in the attempt. Crafting spells adds to Wizardry skill. See [Spells](/spells) for spell information.

## Medallion Crafting

Bolt medallions can be crafted at the [Anvil](/anvil) using 4 Medallion Fragments [![Medallion Fragment](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c6/Medallion_Fragment.png/revision/latest?cb=20180213235033)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Medallion_Fragment)at Wizardry level 25 with a 25% success rate giving 100 exp to craft, 50 exp if it fails.

To upgrade a medallion you need to use a [Weapon Enchant Scroll](/enchanting) at the [Anvil](/anvil) for Bolt to Missile to Wave to Blast.

| Medallion | Low | Medium | High | Superior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bolt to Missile | 20% | 40% | 60% | 70% |
| Missile to Wave | 5% | 20% | 30% | 45% |
| Wave to Blast | 5% | 20% | 30% | 45% |

Up to three [Orbs of Luck](/orb-of-luck) [![Orb Of Luck](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/ad/Orb_Of_Luck.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232512)](/orb-of-luck) can be used to increase chance of success by 10% each. All bolt, missile, wave and blast medallions drop from Mobs. See in game **/wiki mob item medallion** for a list.
