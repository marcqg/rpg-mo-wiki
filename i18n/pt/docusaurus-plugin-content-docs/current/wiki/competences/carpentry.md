## **Carpentry**

Carpentry is a skill that allows players to craft objects for their [Player Island](/player-island). Everything crafted via Carpentry is done in the build menu on the island using materials from the player's chest.

The main skills that give materials for potential carpenters to use are [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) which supplies the logs used, to a lesser extent, [Forging](/forging) which supplies the metal bars for making [Walls](/walls) and [Farming](/farming) for the Wheat and Hay used to make [Breeding Nests](/breeding-nest).

To begin training your carpentry skill you first need to buy an [Island Deed](/island-deed). These can be bought from the Farmer NPC in [Dorpat City](/dorpat) or any other farmer. It functions as a permanent teleport and will send you to your personal island when you equip it within a town or certain other chest locations. This is also the first step necessary to train [Farming](/farming), [Breeding](/breeding) and [Fletching](/fletching).

[![Build menu](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/ff/Build_menu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/122?cb=20250501183601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/ff/Build_menu.png/revision/latest?cb=20250501183601)

A player will find that while he is on the Island where player owned houses can be built, he has a build menu icon on the top right of his screen. This is what allows you to make carpentry items and train carpentry.

Crafting any type of [Chest](/chest) will give you access to your main inventory and [Market](/market-guide).

A player has the ability to build [Floors](/floors), [Walls](/walls) (which include Fences), and [Objects](/objects) of any log type. Oak and higher level walls also require metal bars to be crafted using [Forging](/forging) plus [Sand](/sand) for [Window walls](/walls). Use the links or check in-game wiki for level and material requirements of different items (/wiki craft skill carpentry). Also the \[All Formulas\] button in the Build Menu under each heading shows all items, materials and level needed to craft until you have reached that level.

Because fir logs give very little experience, it is recommended to train carpentry with [captcha](/captcha) points to at least level 10 to be able to use Cactus Logs. [Building your Ship](/ship-building), especially during a 2x event, is also a great way to level your carpentry early on and a [Ship](/ship-building) will let you visit other players islands for ideas.
