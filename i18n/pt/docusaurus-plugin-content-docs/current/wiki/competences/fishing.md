To begin fishing, you need to equip a fishing tool. The most basic is the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) [![Fishing Rod](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)

If you didn't get one from [Tutorial Island](/tutorial-island), one can be purchased from the [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master) [![Dorpat Fishing Master](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156) near the [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611) (Dorpat 12,12) for 40 coins.

[![Screenshot 2015-05-12 at 8.30.46 PM](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/43/Screenshot_2015-05-12_at_8.30.46_PM.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20150513013333)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/43/Screenshot_2015-05-12_at_8.30.46_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20150513013333)

[![Screenshot 2015-05-12 at 8.30.46 PM](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/43/Screenshot_2015-05-12_at_8.30.46_PM.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/267?cb=20150513013333)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/43/Screenshot_2015-05-12_at_8.30.46_PM.png/revision/latest?cb=20150513013333)

Next, select the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) in your [Inventory](/inventory) to equip it. It will now have a yellow box around it. Then, click on an appropriate fishing spot to begin filling your [Inventory](/inventory). Some are located at the Docks to your west, keyboard letter 'w', 'left arrow' or click on the docks you see in the upper left corner. Fishing will continue until your [Inventory](/inventory) is full or you move your away from the fishing spot. Talk to the [Fishing Instructor](/fishing-instructor) [![Fishing Instructor](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/69/Fishing_Instructor.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223127)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/69/Fishing_Instructor.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223127) for more help.

NOTE: When you get a [pet](/pets), you will need to manually transfer your inventory into the [pets'](/pets) inventory. If you have enabled the 'Keybinding Extensions' [mod](/mods) and assigned keyboard keys, you can load or unload your pets' inventory with a single keystroke.

You can sell up to Level 30 raw fish (and [Bass](/raw-bass)) to the [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master) [![Dorpat Fishing Master](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156) or you can take it to the [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611), select a type of fish and cook all of that type in your [Inventory](/inventory). Each type of fish needs to be selected individually as some require a higher [Cooking](/cooking) level. Cooked fish sells for more coins and also becomes edible if you need to replenish your [health](/health) after battle but [cooking](/cooking) is not always successful and you may turn 10 raw fish into 3 or 4 (or less) cooked fish. Then again, if you don't cook anything, your [cooking](/cooking) level won't increase.

See also [Fishing](/fishing-new) sub page.

See [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master) and [Cesis Fishing Master](/cesis-fishing-master) for their buy/sell listing.
