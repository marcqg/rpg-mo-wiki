MOS Market is a Premium in-game Store that uses MOS. MOS is the premium currency of RPG MO and is purchased with real money for in-game benefits. For Example $1 is 100 MOS.You are able to trade MOS purchased items to other players using the [Market](/market-guide) in your chest. It is also earned from [Daily Rewards](/daily-rewards), [Party Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) rewards, [Kill Quest](/quests) rewards and [Skill Quest](/skill-quests) rewards as well as Patreon bonus rewards.
