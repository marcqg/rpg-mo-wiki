## Use

At lvl 10 [Jewelry](/jewelry) you can combine 2x **Silver Chunks** at a [Furnace](/furnace) to make Silver Bar.

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 25 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Dorpat](/dorpat),[Moche I](/moche-i),[Jewelry Guild](/jewelry-guild),[Narwa](/narwa),[Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest),[Reval](/reval),[Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle)

Illusion Guild.

Fastest way is mining from [Dorpat](/dorpat).
