_**Skill quests**_

[![Ss+(2017-02-13+at+05.28.50)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Ss%2B%282017-02-13%2Bat%2B05.28.50%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/320?cb=20170224155053)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Ss%2B%282017-02-13%2Bat%2B05.28.50%29.png/revision/latest?cb=20170224155053)

Skill quests can be taken at any [Quest Master](/quest-master) NPC. They are based on the various Crafting and Gathering [Skills](/skills) available ingame. Players can have only 1 active skill quest at a time, and taking a new one will discard the current active skill quest regardless of progress.

10 quests are shown to player, and are refreshed every 60 minutes. Each cycle contains a maximum of 2 quests from the same skill. The quests can be manually refreshed by using 3 [Repeat Quest Permissions](/repeat-quest-permission)

Quests will automatically complete once the indicated amount of resources have been collected/crafted. Coin and exp rewards are awarded immediately, and the exp rewards are affected by any 2X events that are active. Item rewards will be placed in the player's inventory or their market transactions it's full.

Quests may award you with items, mos, exp or coins and have different grades (Common/Rare/Legendary) which determine the value of the rewards. Skill quests also reward you with points, which determine how often rare or legendary quests appear, up to a maximum % rate.
