> 🇵🇱 Wersja polska — <a href="/rpg-mo-wiki/walls">see the English page</a>

Walls are any item listed on the Build Menu on your [Island](/island-deed) under the heading 'Walls'. These include walls, window walls and fences. All objects are made with materials kept in your [Chest](/chest) until placed on your [Island](/island-deed).

When you Make items, many of them can be rotated before you Place them to change the direction they face, Walls face three directions and Window Walls have multiple images, OR after you Place them you can rotate them where they are by right clicking on item. Very helpful for orienting fences as well as not having to empty a cabinet to turn it.

See also [Floors](/floors) and [Objects](/objects).

## Walls

Walls can be Rotated either before you Place them or right click an existing wall to Rotate it. They have three orientations to complete the exterior of your house. Each wall takes up one square of your island so Objects Placed next to a wall may be some distance away from that wall.

## Fences/Gates

Fences are only made with Fir Logs and Iron Bars but can be Rotated in many directions for a unique look to your breeding area or even a flower garden. They do not need to look like a real fence, end to end, but each can be turned in its own direction and still make a continuous [Breeding](/breeding) pen.

Gate allows you to restrict access through the Gate on your island. Only those listed, up to 5 names, can enter. You must block off the surrounding area for it to be effective.
