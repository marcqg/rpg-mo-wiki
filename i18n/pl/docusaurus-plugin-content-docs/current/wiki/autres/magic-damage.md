Magic Damage is the damage given to an enemy using a Spell, or Magical Weapon. Magic Damage can also be taken from an enemy.

Both magic block and modified defense of the defender reduce the magic damage by the following formula:

(Magic Damage \* min(1, \[Magic + Spell Penetration\]/Defense) ) \* (1 - Magic Block%/100)
