"Explore Explorers", is the 13th quest offered in the game of RPG MO. The player must kill 35 [Explorers](/explorer) to complete the quest, and will be given 1 of 3 possible rewards at random.

| Rewards |
| --- |
| 2 [MOS](/mos) |
| 266 Exp |
| 874 [Coins](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Coin) |
