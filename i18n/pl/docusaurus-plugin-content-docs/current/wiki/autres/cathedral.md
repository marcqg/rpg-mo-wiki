**Getting there:** Take the [Illusion Gate](/illusion-gate) in east [Walco](/walco) to [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle) then take the eastern or lower gates 4 times until you get to [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest) and the Cathedral entrance will be in the south east corner of the map. Cathedral is a type of personal dungeon/boss run with 100 floors, the goal is to get as far as you can with only what you bring into Cathedral. All mobs have 100% [Archery](/archery) block.

Floors are broken into sets of 10, the progression of each set is

*   floors 1-4: progressively harder monsters
*   floor 5: mini-boss
*   floors 6-9: progressively harder monsters
*   floor 10: main boss

## Notes

If you are disconnected for ANY reason, you must log back on to the same world you were on to continue in Cathedral.

The mini and main bosses have a high drop rate of items, with a lot of high-value items.

Monsters in Cathedral do not drop holiday items.

This is a [Death](/death)\-safe zone, there is no penalty for death.

There is a cool-down timer of 48 hours for Cathedral. You can use [Repeat Quest Permissions](/repeat-quest-permission) to reduce this timer.

*   1 RPQ = -60min

The 41st room has an NPC called 'Survivor' that sells Cooked Swordfish and [Cooked Lobsters](/cooked-lobster). Stock up before continuing past him.

Cathedral has a ranking for what floor players have made it to. For players that made it to the same floor it lists them in order of quickest person to make it there.

Currently (Oct. 2017) there are 204 people who have made it above floor 100 [\[1\]](http://mo.ee/highscore.html).
