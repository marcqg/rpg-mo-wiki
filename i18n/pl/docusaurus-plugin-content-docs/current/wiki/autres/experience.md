The total **experience** needed to go from level 1 to n is the same for every skill ([experience chart](/experience-chart)), but the rate at which the experience is gained might vary significantly. Experience can be obtained by practicing the corresponding skill, by dealing damage (for [accuracy](/accuracy), [strength](/strength), [defense](/defense), [health](/health) and [Archery](/archery)) or by [special rewards](/special-rewards). Players can also trigger [2x experience events](/2x-experience-event) for all other players by consuming [2x Experience Potions](/2x-experience-potion). [Magic](/magic) gains experience from casting spells, [crafting spells](/spell-crafting) and gear and using [Teleport Scrolls](/teleport-scrolls).

## Combat skills

For the combat skills (except [magic](/magic)), the experience given is proportional to the physical damage dealt by the player. For a damage of n health points, the experience is distributed among the combat skills depending on the fight mode selected by the player, according to the table below:

| Fighting Mode | Experience given to | Adjusted Skills | Pet Exp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Strength | Defense | Health |
| Controlled | (2/3)x | (2/3)x | (2/3)x | 1x | \- | 3x |
| Accurate | 2x | \- | \- | 1x | Acc +1 | 3x |
| Aggressive | \- | 2x | \- | 1x | Str +1 | 3x |
| Defensive | \- | \- | 2x | 1x | Def +1 | 3x |
| Cordial | \- | \- | \- | 2x | Acc/Def/Str -1 | 2x |

Some special experience rewards, like the quest rewards, will be distributed among the combat skills according to the same table above (with the reward being equal to 3n).

Magic experience is obtained by casting a spell or using a teleport scroll or teleport book.

## Special rewards

Experience can also be obtained by special rewards, like:

*   Spending [captcha](/captcha) points
*   Completing a [quest](/quests)
*   Completing a ["kill" party dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons#"Kill"_Dungeons)
*   Earning [daily rewards](/daily-rewards)
