Template:Infobox Item Jute Fabric is a material used in crafting Magic Armor, Spells and Archery Bows. [Tossa Jute Seed](/tossa-jute-seed), which grows Jute Fabric, can be purchased from [Wandering Farmer](/wandering-farmer) for 3801 coins.

Here is a list of items you can craft using Silk Fabric in your [Inventory](/inventory). Only the fabric and thread is used during the crafting. NOTE: Silk Fabric is not used when making the Merlin Hat. NOTE: Some of the crafted items are also loot drops from [Mobs](/monsters).
