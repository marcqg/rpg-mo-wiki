Back item, obtained by enchanting [Dragon Wings +5](/dragon-wings) . Can be enchanted up to +9, at +10 turns into [War Dragon Wings +10](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/War_Dragon_Wings) or Peace Dragon Wings +10 .

## Blaze Dragon Wings +6

Requires 60 Defense to wear, offfers 90 armor.

## Blaze Dragon Wings +7

Requires 70 Defense to wear, offfers 100 armor.

## Blaze Dragon Wings +8

Requires 80 Defense to wear, offfers 130 armor.

## Blaze Dragon Wings +9

Requires 90 Defense to wear, offers 160 armor.
