Moche II is accessible from Reval via Traveller's Sign between Dorpat's Traveller's Sign and Reval Town. It is the sign that is closest to Reval (the other one closer to the Dorpat Traveller's Sign will bring you to Moche I, not II)

A fletching table is located in the northwest area of the map.(21,87)
