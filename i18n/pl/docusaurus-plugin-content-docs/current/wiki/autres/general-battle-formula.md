*   +3 Armor = +1 Def.
*   +2 Aim. = +1 Acc.
*   +5 Str. _or_ +10 Power = +1 max. melee dmg.
*   +1 equipped Magic = +0.83 Magic skill (5/6 fraction)

## Compare player offensive stats versus enemy defensive stats:

Melee:

| Acc. > Def. | 100% melee hit rate & from 2 dmg. to 100% of max. melee dmg. |
| --- | --- |
| Acc. &lt; Def. | Melee hit rate reduced & overall melee dmg. reduced |

Magic:

| Magic > Def. | 100% spell hit rate & 25–100% spell dmg. |
| --- | --- |
| Magic &lt; Def. | Never miss & overall spell dmg. reduced |

Archery:

| Arch. > Def. | 100% arrow hit rate & ??%–100% arrow dmg. |
| --- | --- |
| Arch. &lt; Def. | Arrow hit rate reduced & overall arrow dmg. reduced |

DAMAGE:

*   Melee - can be raised up by str and power
*   Spell - has own damage _(but dps. can be raised up by shorter cooldowns)_
*   Arrow - has own damage _(but its dmg. can be raised up by ex. bow +x% modifiers)_
