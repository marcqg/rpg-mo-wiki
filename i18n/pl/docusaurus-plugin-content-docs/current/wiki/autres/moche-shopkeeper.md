The _Moche Shopkeeper_ sells various basic items. He can be located in the City of Moche with coordinates of (51,64). [Moche](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Moche) can be accessed by using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in the most north-west corner of [Dorpat](/dorpat), or by using the Travelers Sign in south-west [Reval](/reval).
