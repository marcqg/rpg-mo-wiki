The _Dungeon_ is accessible from a variety of locations in the world of [Dorpat](/dorpat). The Dungeon contains various monsters and various mine-able ores.

Check also all the [Locations](/locations).

## Entrances

*   The Rat Cave is in the northern tip of [Dorpat](/dorpat), to the North-West of the Blacksmith ([Dorpat](/dorpat) 9, 25). [Copper](/copper), [Tin](/tin), and [Clay](/clay) [Mining](/mining) nodes are available within, along with a [Cage](/cage) [Fishing](/fishing) spot.
*   The [Mining Guild](/mining-guild) to the south-east ([Dorpat](/dorpat) 56, 14) requires a [Mining Guild Permission](/mining-guild-permission) to be equipped (65 [Mining](/mining)) to access.
*   Skeleton Cave, ([Dorpat](/dorpat) 66, 29) West of Dorpat Outpost contains [Iron](/iron) and [Iron Vein](/iron-vein) as long as you avoid the Skeleton and Vampire.
*   The [Behemoth](/behemoth) Pit can be entered through the [Minotaur](/minotaur) Maze in the far north-west ([Dorpat](/dorpat) 25, 88). In the little lake surrounded by Ruby Dragon, you can fish with [Steel Harpoon](/steel-harpoon)
*   Under the Paladin's Castle ([Dorpat](/dorpat) 42, 56), you can find their Basement.
