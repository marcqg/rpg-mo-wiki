## Use

At lvl 25 [Jewelry](/jewelry) you can combine 2x **Gold Chunks** at a [Furnace](/furnace) to make [Gold Bar](/gold-bar).

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 45 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon),[Reval](/reval),[Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest),[Blood River](/blood-river),[Hell](/hell),[Jewelry Guild](/jewelry-guild).

Fastest way is mining from [Jewelry Guild](/jewelry-guild).

Below [Jewelry](/jewelry) lvl 60 you can mine it in [Reval](/reval) but you have to kill a [Fire Viper](/fire-viper).
