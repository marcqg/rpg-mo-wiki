The 2x experience points event is a expanse of time which gives the player twice the amount of experience for all stats than you would normally earn from that activity. 2x experience events happen when a player uses a [2x Experience Potion](/2x-experience-potion). The event affects everyone on at the time, and it lasts 30 minutes, unless multiple potions are used.
