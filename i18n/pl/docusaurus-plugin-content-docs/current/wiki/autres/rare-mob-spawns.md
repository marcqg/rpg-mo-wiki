[![Rare Hunting Reward Chest](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f7/Rare_Hunting_Reward_Chest.png/revision/latest?cb=20181030231756)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f7/Rare_Hunting_Reward_Chest.png/revision/latest?cb=20181030231756)

Rare mobs now spawn on most maps at any unoccupied spaces at a rate from every 30 minutes to every 2 hours. Each mob drops a key [![Rare Key](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/1e/Rare_Key.png/revision/latest?cb=20181102070419)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Rare_Key) that can be used at the [Tavern](/tavern) Rare Hunting Reward Chest[![Rare Hunting Reward Chest-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Rare_Hunting_Reward_Chest-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20181102071340)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Rare_Hunting_Reward_Chest). The keys range from 10, 30, 60 and 80 health and determines the level of item you receive. **You must be healed up to the level of the key before you can use them not just have the skill.** Keys cannot be sold to other players, staked in duels and if you die in No Man's Land with a key it is not transferred to the other player but is destroyed if not a safe item. They can be sold to a General Shopkeeper at 8% value or to NML Shopkeeper for 40% value.

[![Rares](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Rares.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/679?cb=20181107035033)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Rares.png/revision/latest?cb=20181107035033)
