## Creatures

Creatures are various lifeforms that inhabit RPG MO. Creatures can also be known as [NPCs](/npcs). Like NPCs, creatures are not human controlled, and depending on the type of NPC, may be hostile or passive.

## Monsters

[Monsters](/monsters) are hostile creatures or NPCs that a player can fight for loot and experience.
