The power orb is a magic weapon available at level 50 magic.

It has +121 aim +1 strength +101 magic and is equipped in the left hand.

It is dropped from World tree, [Titan Minotaur](/titan-minotaur), The Reaper, Pharoah, Orc Overlord, Nephilim Warrior, Diablo, Demon Portal, Demon Overlord, Chaotic Dragon, Cannibal Plant, Blood Eagle, Ancient Hydra and Acid Dragon Lord.

It is crafted into Flash Blast Orb, Air Blast Orb, Earth blast Orb, Water Blast Orb, Fire Blast Orb and

Death Blast Orb.
