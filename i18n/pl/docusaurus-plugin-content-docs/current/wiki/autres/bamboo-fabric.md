Template:Infobox Item Bamboo Fabric is a material used in crafting Magic Armor, Spells and Archery Bows. It is also used in making your [Ship](/ship-building). Can be purchased from [Reval Magician](/reval-magician) for 896 coins.

Here is a list of items you can craft using Bamboo Fabric in your [Inventory](/inventory). Only the fabric and thread is used during the crafting. NOTE: Some of the crafted items are also loot drops from [Mobs](/monsters).
