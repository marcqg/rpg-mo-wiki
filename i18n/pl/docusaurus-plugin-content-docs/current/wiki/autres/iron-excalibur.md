## Statistics of Iron Excalibur

Template:Infobox Item

**Iron Excalibur** is a two-handed sword that can be [forged](/forging) by players or dropped by certain enemies. Normal and Enchanted version can be found in Good and Great Presents.

The Iron Excalibur can be sold to the [Reval Blacksmith](/reval-blacksmith) for half value.

Enchanted Iron Excalibur can be sold to the [Cesis Weapon Merchant](/cesis-weapon-merchant) for half value..

## Stats

**Requirements:**

*   20 Strength
*   Two-handed

**Stats:**

*   +17 Aim
*   +87 Power
*   Value: 15,322

**Enchanted:**

*   +25 Aim
*   +109 Power
*   Value: 28,344

## Dropped by

*   [Skeleton Lord](/skeleton-lord)
*   [Polar Bear](/polar-bear)
*   [Ettin King](/ettin-king)
*   [Emerald Dragon](/emerald-dragon)
