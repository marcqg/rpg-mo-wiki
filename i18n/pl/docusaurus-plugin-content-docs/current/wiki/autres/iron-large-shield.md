The large iron shield can be obtained through forging, Craftable at level 38. It requires x8 Iron bars and x4 Oak Logs. The crafting formula is shown below.

[![Iron Large Shield Formula](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/12/Iron_Large_Shield_Formula.png/revision/latest?cb=20150322114838)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/12/Iron_Large_Shield_Formula.png/revision/latest?cb=20150322114838)

  

  

  

  

When equipped the shield provides +40 armor and requires 20 defense.

Buyer/Seller Reval Blacksmith

Value 7,660
