1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw FishRaw Swordfish is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool), and can be cooked into a [Swordfish](/swordfish). The Raw Swordfish cannot be consumed. Raw Swordfish requires level 50 cooking to use.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Skeletal Dragon](/skeletal-dragon)
*   [Narwa](/narwa) ([Fishing](/fishing) level 50-55) via [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon)
