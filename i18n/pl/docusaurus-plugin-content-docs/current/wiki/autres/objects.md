Objects are any item listed on the Build Menu on your [Island](/island-deed) under the heading 'Objects'. These include furniture for your island, Trophies, Tombstones, fake trees for decorative purposes and Cathedral furniture such as [Piano](/piano), Grandfather Clock etc. All crafted objects are made with materials kept in your [Chest](/chest) until placed on your [Island](/island-deed).

When you 'Make' items, many of them can be rotated before you 'Place' them to change the direction they face, Chairs face four directions and cabinets/beds/tables etc face two, OR after you Place them you can rotate them where they are by right clicking the item. Very helpful for orienting fences as well as not having to empty a cabinet to turn it.

Crafted objects are listed here. For non-crafted use the Build - Objects menu for 'All formulas' on your island.

See aso [Walls](/walls) and [Floors](/floors).
