Health is a [Skill](/skills) that increases the amount of damage you can take before you die and is a requirement for [Equipment](/equipment), primarily for [Jewelry](/jewelry) and [Pets](/pets).  

Health is restored by consuming [Food](/foods). Health cannot be restored above it's maximum value with food.

## Effect on Combat

If a players health reaches zero they [Die](/death).

Food cannot be eaten during combat to restore health, you must run away first.

## Notes

*   Health regenerates at a rate of 1 per minute
*   Health does not regenerate while you are offline.
*   Health gains experience through [Physical Damage](/physical-damage) and rewards exp like [Daily](/daily-rewards), [Party Quests](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) and [Kill Quests](/quests).
*   [Cordial Fighting Mode](/fighting-mode) gives 2 Health exp points per melee damage done.
