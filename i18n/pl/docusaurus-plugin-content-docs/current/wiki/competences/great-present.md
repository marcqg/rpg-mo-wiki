> 🇵🇱 Wersja polska — <a href="/rpg-mo-wiki/great-present">see the English page</a>

Available from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) for 100 MOS or from your Achievement Rewards for 50 points, this item [![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729) contains a random item with a value up to 170,000 coins. Equip it in your [Inventory](/inventory) to receive your gift.

Four Great Presents can be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to make a [![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728) [Best Present](/best-present) that contains a random item with a value up to 450,000 coins.

Four [Good Presents](/good-present) can be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to make a [![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](http://rpg-mo.wikia.com/wiki/File:Great_Present.png) Great Present.

Crafting of presents is 100% successful.
