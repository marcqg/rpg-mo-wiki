There are three Towers in [Void I](/void-i). [Nature Tower](/nature-tower), [Ice Tower](/ice-tower) and [Fire Tower](/fire-tower). Find these towers in the center of [Void I](/void-i) by entering [Void I](/void-i) from just outside [Clouds](/clouds) city entrance or south east of the Fire Altar in [Blood River](/blood-river) by using the [Illusion Gates](/illusion-gate) located there.

## Nature Tower

[![Nature Tower](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/3c/Nature_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202702)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/3c/Nature_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202702)

For **[Nature Tower](/nature-tower)** it is recommended you have base combat stats in the 70s, with appropriate gear.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding Gate is 108,180. Assuming a combat stance other than Cordial, finishing Nature Tower will grant you a total of 233,960 combat experience for your current stance. This includes the 400 bonus experience per room. 350,940 Exp total.

## Ice Tower

[![Ice Tower](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/57/Ice_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202631)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/57/Ice_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202631) For **[Ice Tower](/ice-tower)** it is recommended you have base combat stats in the 80s, with appropriate gear.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding Deadly Tree is 427,750. Total Bonus experience is 132,375. Assuming a combat stance other than Cordial, finishing Ice Tower will grant you a total of 943,750 combat experience for your current stance, 1,415,625 combat experience in total. This includes the bonus experience for each room. Using Magic will change mob exp gained.

## Fire Tower

[![Fire Tower](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c4/Fire_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202517)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c4/Fire_Tower.png/revision/latest?cb=20260223202517) For **[Fire Tower](/fire-tower)** it is recommended you have base combat stats in the 95s, with appropriate gear.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding Gate is 686700. Total Bonus experience is 216,000. Assuming a combat stance other than Cordial, finishing Fire Tower will grant you a total of 1,589,400 combat experience for your current stance. This includes the bonus experience for each room. Using Magic will change mob exp. The total exp for fire is 2,384,100.

\-----------

**All Towers**

Each run through all 3 towers will yield 4,150,665 exp plus a small amount from hit on the final, infinite strength mob at the end of each tower. Using magic will change the amount of exp gained.
