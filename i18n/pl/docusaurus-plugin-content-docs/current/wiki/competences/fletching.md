Fletching is the skill used to craft [Archery](/archery) equipment such as arrows, bows, quivers and armor.

## Fletching Table [![Fletching Table](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e8/Fletching_Table.png/revision/latest?cb=20170903061540)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e8/Fletching_Table.png/revision/latest?cb=20170903061540) 

A [Fletching Table](/fletching-table) is required to craft arrows and must be placed on your [Island](/player-island). It can be made with level 65 [Carpentry](/carpentry) using 1000 [Bronze Bars](/bronze-bar), 180 [Iron Bars](/iron-bar), and 500 [Maple Logs](/maple-log) from your Island Build - Objects menu. If your Carpentry level is not high enough to make one, another player may make a fletching table for you if you provide them with materials. There is a fletching table in [Moche II](/moche-ii) at coordinates (23,89). The closest [Chest](/chest) is in [Reval](/reval). There is one in the [Forest Master Guild](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_Master_Guild_-_(Fletching_Guild)) and in [Sylvanus' Illusion Guild](/illusion-guild-silvanus).

### Arrows

Arrows are crafted at a [Fletching Table](/fletching-table) and have a 100% success rate. They are made from logs, feathers, and either bars or cut gems, such as [cut crystals](/cut-crystal). The [Archery](/archery) level required for arrows depends solely on the log type (level 1 for fir, 10 for cactus, 20 for oak, 30 for willow, 40 for maple, 50 for spirit, 60 for blue palm, 75 for magic oak, 90 for void, and 105 for dragon's blood).
