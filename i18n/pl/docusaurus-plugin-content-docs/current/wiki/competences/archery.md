Archery is probably the least practiced combat [Skill](/skills), given how expensive arrows are and how difficult it is to acquire a [Fletching Table](/fletching-table) if crafting one's own arrows with [Fletching](/fletching). The main appeal of Archery is that it is the only ranged combat skill, meaning one is hardly ever hit by monsters. Archery is practiced by equipping a bow and quiver, filling the quiver with arrows, and shooting what you feel like. Arrows are lost, which is why Archery can be quite expensive.

## Archery Damage

Maximum archery damage depends on your arrows (and adjusted with bows and capes), not archery level. As long as the player's modified archery level is higher than the enemy's defense, they can deal max damage and having higher archery is not useful. Archery skill gains 2 points per damage done. Killing a mob in any of the [Towers](/tower) adds the room bonus to Archery skill.

The players modified archery stat is found by taking the total archery stat from gear, dividing it by 2, and then adding the players archery level, or

gear archery/2 + archery level= modified archery

## Equipment

Mainly directed to new players, items in tables with red backgrounds are from the Monster Book drops or relics. They might have better stats than other options for your current level, but are very hard to get and thus very expensive. So unless you already have those items, just look for the next best item for you. :D

Note that no event items are listed, even though some might be equal or better than the normal options, since they can be inordinately expensive. If you are looking for a bow at lower levels, make sure to check out the items from the previous event in case one fits your needs!

### Bows

Bows are held in the left hand and are crafted with [Fletching](/fletching) skill at an [Anvil](/anvil). The Firelord bows can be made by [Enchanting](/enchanting) the Firelord Shortbow and Firelord Longbow that can be obtained from the [MOS Market](/mos-market). Shortbows are longbows are significantly different in stats, so it is important to make sure you have the proper weapon. The most basic explanation is this:

**Shortbows for leveling archery, longbows for PVP.**

### Arrows

Arrows are crafted with [Fletching](/fletching) at a [Fletching Table](/fletching-table), generally requiring logs, bars or gems, and feathers. The log type decreases cooldown time (increasing damage done per time), feather type increases speed and range, and bar/gem type increases maximum damage.

#### Number of uses

The number of uses depends on the overall quality of the materials used for the crafting and whether a NPC sells the arrow (then the uses are reduced to half the expected value).

_Note:_ Since the formula for this stat is a bit technical it is hidden by default.
