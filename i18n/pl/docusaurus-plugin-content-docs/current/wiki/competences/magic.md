Magic is a [Skill](/skills) that effects [Magic Damage](/effective-magic) and is a requirement for [Equipment](/equipment) dealing with [Spell](/spells) casting.

There are 3 main types available as you mentioned: Wands, Staves, Orbs.

*   **Orbs** provide the largest amount of Aim and No cooldown reduction.
*   **Wands** _(scepters)_ provide higher Aim, Medium amount of cooldown reduction.
*   **Staves** provide the lowest amount of Aim, and the Highest amount of cooldown reduction.

[https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=7&t=8477](https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=7&t=8477) Useful forum post regarding Magic, its benefits and limitations.

Indepth look at Magic costs and Levels, ☀[https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=3](https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=3)

To cast spells you need to equip a [Magic Pouch](/magic-pouches) or Magic Book. This goes in your offhand (right hand), so you are not able to use a shield or 2h weapon with spells. Once you have equipped a spell pouch, you are able to place spells inside the pouch by clicking spell scrolls in your inventory. Right click and 'Use All' to load all of that spell into the pouch/book.

At first you start out with 1 spell slot but higher level pouches can hold more spells.

[![Spells](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/88/Spells.png/revision/latest?cb=20170920003149)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/88/Spells.png/revision/latest?cb=20170920003149)

When in combat, you can cast a spell by clicking the spell's icon

or by pressing the corresponding button on your keyboard (1, 2, 3, or 4). The Auto Cast mod allows for automatic casting of spells.

Spells have a limited quantity per scroll so having more than one in your inventory to replace your current spell makes sense.

## Effect on Combat

Magic level acts similar to accuracy for a spells damage. Instead of a chance to miss, the maximum damage each spell can do is reduced if your opponents defense is above your magic stat. It is possible for spell damage to be reduced so low that they hit for 0.

## Notes

[Cooldown](/cooldown) determines how often you can cast a spell.
