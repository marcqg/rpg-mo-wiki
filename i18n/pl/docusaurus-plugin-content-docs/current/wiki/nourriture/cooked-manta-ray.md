**Cooked Manta Ray** is a fish that is obtained by cooking a [Raw Manta Ray](/raw-manta-ray) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire). A player with 58 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 35% chance of successfully cooking the fish; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level. Successfully cooking this fish will grant 136 Cooking [experience](/experience).
