**Cooked Bass** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Bass](/raw-bass) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 40 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

Template:Infobox Item

## Can be Obtained From

*   Cooking [Raw Bass](/raw-bass)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

*   [Spirit](/spirit) (10%)
*   [Shadow Ghost](/shadow-ghost) (10%)
*   [Poltergeist](/poltergeist) (10%)
*   [Ghost](/ghost) (10%)
*   [Energy Ghost](/energy-ghost) (10%)
