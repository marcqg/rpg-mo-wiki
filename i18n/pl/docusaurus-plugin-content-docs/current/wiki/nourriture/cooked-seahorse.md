**Cooked Seahorse** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Seahorse](/raw-seahorse) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 41 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

## Can be Obtained From

*   Cooking [Raw Seahorse](/raw-seahorse)
*   [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Snow Gungan Priest](/snow-gungan-priest) (2%)
*   [Ice Giant](/ice-giant) (2%)
