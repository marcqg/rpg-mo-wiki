## Santa's Sleigh (Ancient)

This pet was added in 2016. It was one of the few 24-slot pets, until Christmas 2023 when it was basically re-added as the Christmas Sleigh, only this time with +30 speed. Unsurprisingly, older players were miffed as this destroyed the value of the pet, which was previously 50m to 100m.
