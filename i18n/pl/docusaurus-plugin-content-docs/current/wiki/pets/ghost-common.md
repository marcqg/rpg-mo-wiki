**Ghost (Common)** is a pet that requires level 10 [Health](/health). This pet can be obtained as a drop from [Barbarian Ghosts](/barbarian-ghost) in [Cesis](/cesis), [Fellin Island](/fellin-island), or Witches I. It can be evolved into a [Nightmare Ghost (Common)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Nightmare_Ghost_(Common)) after gaining 100,000 [experience](/experience) while equipped.
