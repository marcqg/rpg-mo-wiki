The Fire Imp is an aggressive CL 42 NPC monster located in the [Blood River](/blood-river) and [Reval](/reval). It has a moderate resistance to Magic with a Magic Block of 30.

## Quest

"Small Ones Can Burn", is the fourteenth [quest](/quests) in the game. Requiring 40 Fire Imp kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 2 MOS

\- 364 Exp

\- 1,200 Coins

From Reval Town, head north east to find the largest group of Fire Imps. If one reaches the Jewelry Guild, they've gone too far.

In Reval, there are more Fire Imps inside of the POI, "Snake Maze", however reaching them requires fighting through several [Fire Viper](/fire-viper) (CL 58) and [King Cobra](/king-cobra) (CL 72).
