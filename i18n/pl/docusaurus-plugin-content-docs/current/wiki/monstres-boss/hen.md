## Quest

The **Hen** and the [Chicken](/chicken) are both capable of fulfilling the needed kills for the Poultry Slaughtering quest.

"Poultry Slaughtering", is the second kill quest in the game. Requiring 20 kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 1 MOS

\- 20 Exp

\- 65 Coins
