Gate I is in the final room of [Cathedral](/cathedral). Players that reach this room receive one chance to hit the Gate, which will award a [2x Experience Potion](/2x-experience-potion) on a successful kill. Its "undefined" Strength will then kill the player, removing them from the [Cathedral](/cathedral). Gates will refresh to 100% health on maintenance, and will respawn like any other mob when killed.

  
Gate II is in the final room of [Nature](/nature-tower) and [Fire Tower](/fire-tower). Players that reach this room receive one chance to hit the Gate, which will award a [2x Experience Potion](/2x-experience-potion) on a successful kill. Its "undefined" Strength will then kill the player, removing them from the [Nature](/nature-tower) or [Fire Tower](/fire-tower). Gates will refresh to 100% health on maintenance, and will respawn like any other mob when killed.
