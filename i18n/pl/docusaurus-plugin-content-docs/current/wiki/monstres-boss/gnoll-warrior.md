The Gnoll Warrior is an aggressive CL 54 NPC monster located in the [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon).

## Quest

"What Animal Is That" is the nineteenth quest in the game. Requiring 70 Gnoll Warrior kills to complete. Upon completing this quest, the player will be rewarded with one of the following:

*   2 MOS

*   907 Exp

*   2,983 Coins

Players will likely encounter [Hydras](/hydra) (CL 62) and [Vampires](/vampire)  (CL 44) during this quest and should be equipped to deal with such foes.
