The Skeleton Mage is an aggressive CL 37 NPC monster located in the [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) and [Pernau](/pernau). It can cast [Fire Bolt](/fire-bolt) with a Magic of 20.

## Locations

| Location | Quantity | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) | 5 | (87,19) |
| [Pernau](/pernau) | 18 | (88,64) |

[Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon): Find the ladder (Point C, on the World Map) that is southwest of the [Dorpat Outpost](/dorpat). The Dorpat Outpost is in the southeastern region of [Dorpat](/dorpat).

[Pernau](/pernau): Their location is secluded behind two [White Walls](/white-wall) to the right after entering from [Reval](/reval)
