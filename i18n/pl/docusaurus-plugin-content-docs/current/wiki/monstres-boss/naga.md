The Naga is an aggressive CL 62 NPC monster located in the [Clouds](/clouds), [Cesis](/cesis) and [Fellin Island](/fellin-island).

## Quest

"Don't Look Into The Eyes", is the twenty-second quest in the game. Requiring 85 Naga kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 2 MOS

\- 1,183 Exp

\- 3,890 Coins

[Clouds](/clouds): To reach the Naga in the Clouds, walk north from the [Dorpat](/dorpat) entrance. It should be noted that one must be able to defeat [Archangel](/archangel) (CL = 70) and [Observer](/observer) (CL = 74) in order to reach seven Naga. Once at the POI, "Air Altar", the next right ahead will lead you to the group of Naga. There are seven additional Naga near the entrance to Acid Dragon Lord at (58, 30), where one would need to encounter multiple Blood Battle Mage (CL = 81) in order to reach them.

[Fellin Island](/fellin-island): Northwest corner of the map

[Cesis](/cesis): Southeast corner of the map
