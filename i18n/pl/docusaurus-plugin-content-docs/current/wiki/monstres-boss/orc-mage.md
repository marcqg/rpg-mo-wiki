The Orc Mage is an aggressive CL 25 NPC monster located in [Dorpat](/dorpat).

## Quest

"Orc Invasion Part II", is the ninth quest in the game. Requiring 24 Orc Mage kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 1 MOS

\- 144 Exp

\- 472 Coins

[Dorpat](/dorpat): Orc Mages can be found far north of Dorpat Town, just south of the POI, "Minotaur Maze".
