The Cactus can be cut at level 5 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) and above. When cut down successfully, cactus wood is acquired. The value of the cactus wood is 30 coins. The log can be used in the skills of [Carpentry](/carpentry) and [Fletching](/fletching). Template:Infobox Tree

## Island Decoration

A cactus can also be purchased as a decoration for your [Island](/island-deed) but cannot be used for logs there.

Have the Cactus in your chest and use Build - Objects menu to Place it. Rotate the cactus when Placing it to change the appearance and size of the cactus.

[Dorpat Decorations Shopkeeper](/dorpat-decorations-shopkeeper) - 25,000 coins.
