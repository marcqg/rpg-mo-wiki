To begin fishing, you need to equip a fishing tool. The most basic is the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) [![Fishing Rod](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)

If you didn't get one from [Tutorial Island](/tutorial-island), one can be purchased from the [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master) [![Dorpat Fishing Master](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156) near the [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611) (Dorpat 18,20) for 40 coins. They are also dropped by [Hens](/hen) [![Hen](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f1/Hen.png/revision/latest?cb=20150401184718)](/hen)or found by breaking [Tools Box](/tools-box) [![Tools Box](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/fc/Tools_Box.png/revision/latest?cb=20171105224822)](/tools-box) under Dorpat near the tin/copper/clay mining or behind the Dorpat Outpost.

Next, select the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) in your [Inventory](/inventory) to equip it. It will now have a yellow box around it. Then, click on an appropriate fishing spot to begin filling your [Inventory](/inventory). Some are located at the Docks to your west, keyboard letter 'a', 'left arrow' or click on the docks you see in the upper left corner. Fishing will continue until your [Inventory](/inventory) is full or you move your character away from the fishing spot. Talk to the [Fishing Instructor](/fishing-instructor) [![Fishing Instructor](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/69/Fishing_Instructor.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223127)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/69/Fishing_Instructor.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223127) for more help. Load game [/mods](/mods) and use /find fishing rod for other locations.

See [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master), [Cesis Fishing Master](/cesis-fishing-master) and [Wittensten Fishing Master](/wittensten-fishing-master) for their buy/sell listing.

See [http://www.rpgmobob.com/fishing-spots.html](http://www.rpgmobob.com/fishing-spots.html) for a list of POI locations.

NOTE: Check loot drop rates at a fishing spot by right clicking the spot then clicking Drops. Percentage is based on your character's current [Fishing](/fishing) level.

## Cooking

It is often suggested to cook your fish before selling it, or it will be hard keep your [Cooking](/cooking) skill as high as fishing. To cook fish, equip one raw fish in your inventory and take it to the [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611); this will cook all of that type of fish one at a time. Each type of fish needs to be selected and cooked individually as some require a higher [Cooking](/cooking) level. Another way to make sure your cooking level is as high as your fishing level is to fish when there is no [2x Experience Event](/2x-experience-event) , and cook while there is.

## Selling

You can sell up to Level 30 raw fish (and Bass) to the [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master) [![Dorpat Fishing Master](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Fishing_Master.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128223156) . Cooked fish sells for more coins and also becomes edible if you need to replenish your [Health](/health) after battle but [Cooking](/cooking) is not always successful. Raw fish sometimes sells for more coin on the [Player Market](/market-guide), as other players may be looking to level their cooking skill or use them for breeding food.

## Pet

When you get a [Pet](/pets), you will need to manually transfer your inventory into the pet's inventory. If you have enabled the 'Keybinding Extensions' [mod](/mods) and assigned keyboard keys, you can load or unload your pets' inventory with a single keystroke.
