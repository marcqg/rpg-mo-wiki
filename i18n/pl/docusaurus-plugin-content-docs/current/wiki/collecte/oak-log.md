[![Oak Log](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/61/Oak_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114173952)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/61/Oak_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114173952)**Oak Log**  
Requires level 10 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) to obtain from an [Oak Tree](/oak-tree). Successfully cutting an oak log will grant 13 exp per log. Similar to other logs, oak logs are used in other skills, such as [Forging](/forging) (level 20+) and [Carpentry](/carpentry) (level 25). 100 oak logs are also the 3rd resource required in the construction of the [Player ship](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).
