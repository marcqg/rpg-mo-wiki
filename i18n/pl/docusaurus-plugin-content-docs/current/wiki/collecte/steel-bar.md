## Components

Requires in a forge:

*   [![Iron Ore](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Iron_Ore.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211438)](/iron-ore) [Iron Ore](/iron-ore) + [![Coal-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8c/Coal-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211448)](/coal) [Coal](/coal)
*   [Lv 40 Forging skill](/forging)

Has a 40% (base) chance of success. (max 100% @ [Lv 100 Forging](/forging))

## The Best Way to Obtain

The best place you can [craft](/forging) **Steel Bars** is in the [Mining Guild](/mining-guild).

The second best way to obtain the components is quite a bit more Complex:

You will want to start with the hardest to see how well you fare, that would be done by mining the [![Coal-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8c/Coal-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211448)](/coal) [Coal](/coal) from [Rakblood's Town's](/rakblood) [![Coal Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/39/Coal_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143009)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Coal_Vein) Coal Vein. You may need to defeat 2 [![Bronze Golem](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/3d/Bronze_Golem.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202011030)](/bronze-golem) [Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) blocking the road between [Rakblood Town](/rakblood) and the [![Coal Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/39/Coal_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143009)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Coal_Vein) Coal Vein. If you are not high enough [Combat Level to avoid Aggro](/combat-level), you will need to fight up to [![Bronze Golem](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/3d/Bronze_Golem.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202011030)](/bronze-golem) [8 Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) (assuming bad path finding) and [![Iron Golem](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/0d/Iron_Golem.png/revision/latest?cb=20150401184719)](/iron-golem) [1 Iron Golem](/iron-golem) (2 if going for the non-vein [![Coal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/1e/Coal.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143656)](/coal) [Coal](/coal))

The other half of the **Steel Bar** would be the [![Iron Ore](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Iron_Ore.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211438)](/iron-ore) [Iron Ore](/iron-ore), that is a much easier retrieval process, and an extremely detailed explanation is posted in the [![Iron](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8f/Iron.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143655)](/iron) [Iron Mining Node](/iron) and [![file=Iron_Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/Iron_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003134512)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/Iron_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003134512 "file=Iron_Vein") [Iron Vein](/iron-vein)\* pages.

\* Iron Vein to be completed soon

Remember that most [Pets](/pets) will increase your available inventory size when equipped!
