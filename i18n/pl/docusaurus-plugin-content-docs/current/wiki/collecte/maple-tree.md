The _**Maple tree**_ can be cut at level 35 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) and above. When cut down successfully, a [Maple log](/maple-log) is acquired. The standard listed value of a single unit of Maple logs is 180 coins. The log can be used in skills such as [Carpentry](/carpentry), [Forging](/forging), and is a necessary material for player-constructed [ships](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship). ### Location

Maples can be found in:

*   [Reval](/reval) - near the Reval snake maze, to the southeast, near lake with silver ore at (70,17)
*   [Cesis](/cesis) - south-east of Cesis town
