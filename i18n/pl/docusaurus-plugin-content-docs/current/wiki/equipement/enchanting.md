**There are 4 types of items that can be enchanted:**

*   [Weapon](/weapon-list)
*   [Jewelry](/jewelry)
*   [Armor](/armors)
*   [Wing](/wings)

**There are 4 Tiers for each scroll:**

*   [Low Enchanting Scrolls](/low-enchanting-scrolls) - Get from normal weak monsters
*   [Medium Enchanting Scroll](/medium-enchanting-scrolls) - Get from medium- strong monsters
*   High Enchanting Scroll - Get from Weak Boss Monster
*   Superior Enchanting Scroll - Get From Strong Boss Monsters

All are [Mob](/monsters) drops, Bosses, and some are on [MOS Market](/mos-market).

*   Lower grades of enchants can also be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to higher grades.
*   Two different types may be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) into a third type: Med Armor + Med Jewelry = Med Weapon with a chance to fail.

To enchant something you need:

*   The item you want enchanted.
*   The enchant scroll.
*   A [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) equipped.

Click on the [Anvil](/anvil), click the scroll and item to be enchanted and select 'Make', _If unsuccessful all items are lost._

## Gloves Enchanting!

Enchanting Gloves Gives extra stats on aim, power, defence or magic, Depends which way you choose.

*   Sapphire - Defense
*   Ruby - Power
*   Emerald - Magic
*   Diamond- Accuracy
*   Crystal - Archery

Gloves Enchanting will need Magic Runes.

*   Magic Rune \[Common\] +25% success rate
*   Magic Rune \[Rare\] +45% success rate
*   Magic Rune \[Legendary\] +65% success rate
*   Magic Rune \[Ancient\] + 85% success rate

Enchanting items using [Orb Of Luck](/orb-of-luck) [![Orb Of Luck](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/ad/Orb_Of_Luck.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232512)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/ad/Orb_Of_Luck.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232512) adds 10% extra to success rate. 

You can add up to 3 orbs maximum for an additional 30% success chance for enchantments.  

Orbs of Luck are loot drops from Bosses, purchased from the [Market](/chest) or the [MOS Market](/mos-market).

## Experience:

Enchanting gives forging experience. The amount depends on item. This data hasn't been recorded for most items - if you know the amount for other items, please add it:

*   Bronze Full Helmet + Low Armor Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Bronze Full Helmet: 40 experience.
*   Fire Wings + Medium Wings Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Fire Wings: 80 experience.
*   Leather Helmet + Low Armor Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Leather Helmet: 40 experience.
*   Leather Helmet + Medium Armor Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Leather Helmet: 80 experience.
*   Silver Crystal Ring + Low Jewelry Enchant Scroll --> fail: 20 experience.
*   Silver Crystal Ring + Low Jewelry Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Silver Crystal Ring: 40 experience.
*   Wizard Cape + Low Wings Enchant Scroll --> Enchanted Wizard Cape: 40 experience.
*   Wizard Cape + Medium Wings Enchant Scroll --> fail (missed 16%): 40 experience.
