A Cut Crystal [![Cut Crystal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e8/Cut_Crystal.png/revision/latest?cb=20221218043219)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e8/Cut_Crystal.png/revision/latest?cb=20221218043219), made from uncut crystals, can be used to craft [Archery](/archery) equipment, either jewelry or gloves, or [Crystal Wings](/crystal-wings). They are also used in [Breeding](/breeding) to feed certain [Pets](/pets).

## Crafting

### Cut Crystal

A Cut Crystal is made from any grade of uncut crystal (gathered through [Mining](/mining) with a pickaxe or spade) using a [Chisel](/chisel).

| [Jewelry level](/jewelry) | Name | Success Chance | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Uncut Low Grade Crystal](/uncut-low-grade-crystal) | 20% | 20 |
| 1 | Uncut Medium Grade Crystal | 50% | 20 |
| 1 | Uncut High Grade Crystal | 80% | 20 |
| 1 | Uncut Superior Grade Crystal | 100% | 20 |
