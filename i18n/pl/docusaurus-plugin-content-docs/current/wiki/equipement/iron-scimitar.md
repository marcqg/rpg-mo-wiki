An Iron Scimitar is a [Weapon](/weapons) needing level 20 [Accuracy](/accuracy) to wield that can be bought and sold at [Reval Blacksmith](/reval-blacksmith).

Template:Infobox Item +Stats

## [Forging](/forging)

An Iron Scimitar can be forged using 3 [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) and 4 [Oak Logs](/oak-log).
