**Emerald Platemail** is a level 40 [Defense](/defense) chest armor. It can be crafted by a player with 40 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 6 [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) and 3 [Emerald Dragon Scales](/emerald-dragon-scale). The chance of successfully crafting one is 80% and rewards 210 Forging [experience](/experience).

An Emerald Platemail can be enchanted into an Enchanted Emerald Platemail.
