**Bronze Dagger** is a level 1 one-handed weapon. It can be crafted by a player with 1 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) using a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) with 1 [Fir Log](/fir-log) and 2 [Bronze Bars](/bronze-bar). The chance of successfully crafting one is 100% and rewards 25 Forging [experience](/experience). It can be enchanted into an [Enchanted Bronze Dagger](/enchanted-bronze-dagger).
