**Jewelry** is a crafting [skill](/skills) that is used to make rings and necklaces that give bonus stats. See

☀[http://rpg-mo.wikia.com/wiki/Jewelry\_-\_Picture\_Pages](/jewelry-picture-pages) to craft \[R\] jewelry shown in a simple way.

## Crafting

### **Moulds**

Create moulds by equipping each of the Reagents in your inventory and using the Furnace. [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace)  
Kaolinite is for creating a box, or stack, of moulds. Clicking a box expands it into individual moulds.

### **Gems and Bars**

Cut gems in your inventory by equipping the Chisel [![Chisel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Chisel.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215614)](/chisel) and an uncut gem.

Make the bars by equipping the Reagents and using the Furnace. [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace)

Enchant cut gems at the Anvil [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](/anvil) using Jewelry Enchant Scrolls.

## Refined

Refined jewelry is designated with \[R\] after the name and has higher stats than its enchanted version. To craft you begin with an enchanted ring or necklace, add the proper number of enchanted gems, and then use an [Anvil](/anvil) to make with a 75% chance and Forging level 1 for all. These can then be enchanted again for even more benefits, such as magic or melee damage block.

### **Process for Enchanted + Refined Jewelry**

1.  Enchant base ring or necklace with a Jewelry Enchant Scroll.
2.  Refine the _Enchanted_ ring or necklace by using a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) and an [Anvil](/anvil) to combine the _Enchanted_ ring/necklace with refined [Cut](#Gems_and_Bars) gems, such as [Cut Jadeite \[R\]](#Gems_and_Bars) or [Cut Diamond \[R\]](#Gems_and_Bars).
    *   Refined Cut gems can be acquired from other players via the Market or by enchanting cut gems using Jewelry Enchant Scrolls.
3.  Enchant the newly refined ring/necklace for a chance to get an Enchanted + Refined ring/necklace, such as Enchanted Platinum Jadeite Ring \[R\].
