**Fir Log** requires level 1 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) to obtain from a [Fir Tree](/fir-tree) and grant 5 Woodcutting [experience](/experience) each. They are used in various low level [Carpentry](/carpentry), [Forging](/forging), and [Fletching](/fletching) recipes. Fir logs are also used to make Kaolinite Ring Mould Box (R) with [Jewelry](/jewelry), and 300 logs are required as the initial step for builiding the [Player ship](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).
