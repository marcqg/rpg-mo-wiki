**Raw Chicken Leg** is an uncooked food item. A player with level 1 [Cooking](/cooking) can cook it into a Cooked Chicken Leg on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) with a success rate of 50%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping out at level 51 Cooking). Successfully cooking the chicken grants 9 Cooking [experience](/experience).
