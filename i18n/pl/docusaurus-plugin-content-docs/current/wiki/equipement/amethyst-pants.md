Amethyst Pants are a type of [Armor](/armors) that you can wear at level 70 [Defense](/defense), and are also used to make 7 Souls Pants.

Template:Infobox Item +Stats

## Crafting

### Amethyst Pants

Amethyst Pants are made at an [anvil](/anvil) at level 82 [Forging](/forging) (requiring a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer)), giving 320 [Experience](/experience). The items consumed on making are 4 Cut Amethysts and 2 [Fire Stone Bars](/fire-stone-bar). The chance of success stays at 30%.

### 7 Souls Pants

7 Souls Pants can be made at an anvil with level 99 [Forging](/forging).

Materials required:

*   Sapphire Dragon Pants

*   Flame Phoenix Pants

*   Amethyst Pants

*   [Emerald Pants](/emerald-pants)

*   Diamond Pants

*   7 Souls Jewels x3
