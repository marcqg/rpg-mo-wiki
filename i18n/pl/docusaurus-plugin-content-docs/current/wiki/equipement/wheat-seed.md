**Wheat Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 7 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Wheat](/wheat) after 8 minutes. Harvesting the wheat grants 16 Farming [experience](/experience).
