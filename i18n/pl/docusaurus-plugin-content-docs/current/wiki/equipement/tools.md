Tools are required items used in gathering and crafting [Skills](/skills) to perform certain actions.

## [Mining](/mining)

*   [![Spade](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8e/Spade.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215150)](/spade) **:** [Spade](/spade) is used to dig sand.
*   [![Iron Pickaxe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a2/Iron_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203458)](/iron-pickaxe) **:** [Iron Pickaxe](/iron-pickaxe) is required to mine minerals.
*   [![Steel Pickaxe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d1/Steel_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225231857)](/steel-pickaxe) **:** [Steel Pickaxe](/steel-pickaxe) is required to mine mineral veins.
*   [![Mining Guild Permission](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/48/Mining_Guild_Permission.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232633)](/mining-guild-permission) **:** [Mining Guild Permission](/mining-guild-permission) needs to be equipped to enter the [Mining Guild](/mining-guild).

## [Woodcutting](/woodcutting)

*   [![Woodcutter's Axe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/db/Woodcutter%27s_Axe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203421)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe) **:** [Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe) is used for cutting the following Trees:
    *   [Fir Tree](/fir-tree) (Lv1)
    *   [Cactus](/cactus) (Lv5)
    *   [Oak Tree](/oak-tree) (Lv10)
    *   [Willow Tree](/willow-tree) (Lv20)
    *   [Maple Tree](/maple-tree) (Lv35)
*   [![Steel Woodcutter's Axe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a1/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225010636)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe) **:** [Steel Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe) is used for cutting the following Trees:
    *   [Spirit Tree](/spirit-tree) (Lv45)
    *   [Blue Palm](/blue-palm) (Lv55)
    *   [Magic Oak Tree](/magic-oak-tree) (Lv65)
    *   [Void Tree](/void-tree) (Lv85)
    *   [Dragon's Blood Tree](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Blood_Tree) (Lv105)
    *   It can also cut down lower level trees.
*   [![Woodcutting Guild Permission](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e3/Woodcutting_Guild_Permission.png/revision/latest?cb=20170826090740)](/woodcutting-guild-permission) **:** [Woodcutting Guild Permission](/woodcutting-guild-permission) needs to be equipped to enter the [Woodcutting Guild](/woodcutting-guild).

## Other

Some tools are not tied to a specific skill but serve other actions that can not be performed without them.
