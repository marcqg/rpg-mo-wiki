Magic Pouches (or Magic Books) are (right hand) equip-able items required to cast [Spells](/spells) in combat, dealing [Magic Damage](/magic-damage). The tier (Small/Beginner, Medium/Apprentice, Large, Superior, Legendary) and size (S, M, L) of a pouch or book determines its equip level, spell slots, maximum number of spells held, and stat boosts.

[![Example of 2 spells casting simultaneously to add to damage.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/MultiCast.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150904170451)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/MultiCast.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150904170451)

Example of 2 spells casting simultaneously to add to damage.

A spell pouch holds a certain number of casts, based on size. Not just the slots of spell. In addition, if you put multiple types of spells into the pouches, they'll all cast _simultaneously_ (at the rate of individual spell cooldown times). This increases your DPS and your XP/Hour which allows you to take on higher level monsters without being killed before running away.

Base pouches can hold 1,000 casts of a spell per number of slots it has. Thus a Medium Magic Pouch with 2 slots can hold 2000 casts of Earth Missile and 2000 casts of Fire Missile, adding as much as 16 damage per combat round. However, the pouch sizes (S), (M), (L) increases the number of casts held by 1000, 2500 and 5000 respectively.
