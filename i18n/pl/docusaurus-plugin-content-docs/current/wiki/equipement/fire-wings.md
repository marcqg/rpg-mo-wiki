Fire Wings require level 20 [Strength](/strength). They can be enchanted to have the following stats: +19 aim, +19 power and +37 defense (price 86,025).Template:Infobox Item +Stats

## Crafting

Can be crafted at level 30 [Forging](/forging) granting 290 [Experience](/experience), with a constant success rate of 50%.

Materials:

*   [Cut Ruby](/cut-ruby) x5

*   [Gold Bar](/gold-bar) x6

## [Monster](/monsters) Drops

*   \[BOSS\] Chaotic Dragon (4% chance)
