1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw Fish**Raw Seahorse** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Net](/fishing-net), and can be cooked into a [Seahorse](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Seahorse). The Raw Seahorse cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [DarkElf Mage](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/DarkElf_Mage) (5%)
*   [Archdevil](/archdevil) (1%)
