**Hay Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 5 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Hay](/hay) after 6 minutes. Harvesting the hay grants 12 Farming [experience](/experience).
