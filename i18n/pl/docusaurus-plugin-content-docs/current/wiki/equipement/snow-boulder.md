**Snow Boulder** is a 2020, 2021, and 2023 [Christmas Event](/christmas-events) item. It was obtained by Mining with an [Iron Pickaxe](/iron-pickaxe)/[Steel Pickaxe](/steel-pickaxe) or Woodcutting with a [Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe)/[Steel Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe) at most resource locations, or as a potential reward from a [Scavenger Hunt](/scavenger-hunt) for 2020 and 2021, but in 2023 is was also a drop from fishing holes, and not an item in the scavenger hunts.
