A Merlin Hat is a type of [Helm](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Helmets) that has a [Magic](/magic) bonus as well as an [Armor](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Armor) bonus when equipped (requires level 55 [Magic](/magic)).

Template:Infobox Item +Stats

## Crafting

It requires level 65 [Magic](/magic) to craft, and gives 190 [Experience](/experience). The chance of success stays at 50%.

Components:

*   Needle For Hats (not consumed)
*   4 [Jute Fabric](/jute-fabric)
*   3 [Ruby Dragon Scale](/ruby-dragon-scale)
*   1 [Thread](/thread)
