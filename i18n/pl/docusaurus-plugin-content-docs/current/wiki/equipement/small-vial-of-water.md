## Craftable

## Can be Obtained From

Reclaim [Empty Small Vial](/empty-small-vial)[![Empty Small Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/81/Empty_Small_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225162405)](/empty-small-vial) after drinking most small potions.

## Uses

Equip it with an ingredient in your inventory to craft Small sized potions. Requires more than one of a particular ingredient to make a potion and it can fail giving no exp for the attempt. See [Alchemy](/alchemy) for more.

Also used in making [Soil](/soil) for your [Island](/island-deed).
