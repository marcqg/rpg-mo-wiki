**Angel Tree Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 40 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Angel Cotton](/angel-cotton) after 14 minutes. Harvesting the cotton grants 40 Farming [experience](/experience).
