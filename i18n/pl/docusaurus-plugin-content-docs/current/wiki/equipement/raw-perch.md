The **Raw Perch** is one of the first fish available to catch. It can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), and can be cooked into a [Perch](/perch), restoring 3 [Health](/health) upon consumption. The Raw Perch cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)
*   Fishing Off Dorpat Coast

### Drops

*   [Gray Wizard](/gray-wizard) (0.91%)
