**Raw Salmon** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), and can be cooked into a [Salmon](/salmon). The Raw Salmon cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Kobalos](/kobalos) (5%)
*   [Black Rat](/black-rat) (5%)
*   [Grizzly Bear](/grizzly-bear) (2%)
*   [Polar Bear](/polar-bear) (2%)
