The Bronze Golem is an aggressive CL 26 [monster](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Monster) commonly found near mining locations. Though not particularly dangerous, Bronze Golems prove to be a nuisance by attacking lower leveled miners and blocking off mining routes and passages between towns.

At level 46 Bronze Golems are no longer aggressive, making mining in [Rakblood](/rakblood) much more efficient.

## Quest

"Mining Some Golems Part 1" is the tenth kill quest in the game, requiring 27 Bronze Golem kills to complete. Upon completion, the player will be rewarded with either:

*   2 MOS

*   183 Exp

*   603 Coins
