Boss Retaliation is a timed event created three times per day automatically but can also be created by a player using TWO (2) Minigame Scrolls available from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) or sold in the [Player Market](/market-guide) in your chest.

Times of event are 23 - 15 - 7 hours until next **/maintenance** on World 6.

After the chat announcement you have 5 minutes to switch to the proper world and type **/join** in the chat to teleport to the event. This event lasts 10 minutes or less if all boss parts are killed. Multi-combat Block is disabled during these events. To attack with melee/magic you must have access to one of the adjoining squares for each part. Many archers can attack from a distance.

**ITEMS ARE NOT SAFE IF YOU DIE WHILE PARTICIPATING IN THESE EVENTS! Normal item loss rules apply: 2 most expensive /wiki value items and equipped pet OR 7 most expensive /wiki value items and equipped pet with [Potion of Preservation](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_of_Preservation) equipped are safe.**
