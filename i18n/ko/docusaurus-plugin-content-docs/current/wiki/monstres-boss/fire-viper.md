The Fire Viper is an aggressive CL 58 NPC monster located in the [Blood River](/blood-river) and [Reval](/reval).

## Quest

"Snake Game", is the twenty-first quest in the game. Requiring 100 Fire Viper kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 2 MOS

\- 1,152 Exp

\- 3,788 Coins

If in Reval, players will encounter several [King Cobra](/king-cobra) (CL 72) during this quest and should be equipped to deal with such foes.
