The Gnoll Mage is an aggressive CL 43 NPC monster located in the [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon). It has a minor resistance to Magic with a Magic Block of 5, and can cast its own spells with a Magic of 16.

## Quest

"World Is Weird", is the twentieth quest in the game. Requiring 70 Gnoll Mage kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 2 MOS

\- 1,042 Exp

\- 3,424 Coins

Players will likely encounter [Hydra](/hydra) (CL 62) during this quest and should be equipped to deal with such foes.
