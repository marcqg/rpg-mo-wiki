## Quest

The Explorer, is the monster featured in the quest [Explore Explorers](/explore-explorers), and is the 13th quest in the game. Requiring 35 kills to complete, 1 of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random; these will either be...

\- 2 Mos  
\- 266 XP  
\- 874 Coins
