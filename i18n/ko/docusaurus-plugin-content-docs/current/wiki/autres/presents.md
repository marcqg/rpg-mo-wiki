**Presents** are items bought from the MOS store. There are five tiers: good [![Good Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d5/Good_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d5/Good_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728), great[![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729), best[![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728), legendary[![Legendary Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/17/Legendary_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226015901)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/17/Legendary_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226015901), and rare[![Rare Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/aa/Rare_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226015858)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/aa/Rare_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226015858). There are some items that can't be received from presents.
