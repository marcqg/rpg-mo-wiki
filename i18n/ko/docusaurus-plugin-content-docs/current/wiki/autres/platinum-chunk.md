## Use

At lvl 60 [Jewelry](/jewelry) you can combine 4x **Platinum Chunks** at a Furnace to make Platinum Bar.

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 75 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle),[Blood River](/blood-river),[Hell](/hell).
