Emerald Dust is used in the [Alchemy](/alchemy) skill beginning at level 60, and is a necessary ingredient for the _Death Call_ and _Call of Orcs_ potions_._ Emerald Dust at level 30 can be obtained by using an [Emerald Horn](/emerald-horn) with a [Mortar and Pestle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Mortar_and_Pestle).
