To use a **Silver Dragon Scale** (right hand) you need level 28 [alchemy](/alchemy). It can be obtained as a drop from a [Baby Silver Dragon](/baby-silver-dragon) which can be found in [Moche I](/moche-i).

After killing a [Baby Silver Dragon](/baby-silver-dragon) there is a 9% chance that the **Silver Dragon Scale** will be dropped.
