Lost Woods is located through [Whiland](/whiland). Like the name implies, Lost Woods is easy to get lost in as there are no visible paths. The whole of the map has small "sections" which are encompassed by [Fir Tree](/fir-tree). Lost Woods is home to many of the same mobs found in Whiland. It is also home to other mobs such as: [Archdevil](/archdevil), [Ettin King](/ettin-king), and [Behemoth](/behemoth). Three bosses can also be found in Lost Woods: Cave Crawler, Giant Cyclops, and Venus Flytrap.
