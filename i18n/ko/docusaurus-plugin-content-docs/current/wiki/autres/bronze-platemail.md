**Bronze Platemail** is a level 1 [Defense](/defense) chest armor. It can be crafted by a player with 18 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 9 [Bronze Bars](/bronze-bar). The chance of successfully crafting one is 100% and rewards 90 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Bronze Platemail can be enchanted into an Enchanted Bronze Platemail.
