Wells are used to fill empty vials as well as [watering cans](/watering-can) and [multi-tools](/multi-tool).

## Empty vials

These vials can be filled at a well as long as you have required [Alchemy](/alchemy) level to equip it.

*   [Empty Small Vial](/empty-small-vial) (level 1 Alchemy)
*   [Empty Medium Vial](/empty-medium-vial) (Level 30 Alchemy)
*   [Empty Large Vial](/empty-large-vial) (Level 50 Alchemy)
*   [Empty Superior Vial](/empty-superior-vial) (Level 65 Alchemy)

## Locations

Wells are found in [Dorpat](/dorpat), [Moche](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Moche), [Reval](/reval), [Clouds](/clouds), [Fellin Island](/fellin-island), [Star of Knowledge](/star-of-knowledge), Witches II and [Rakblood](/rakblood) which also is the best place to fill lots of vials.
