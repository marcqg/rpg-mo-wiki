## iron veins are the same as iron ore but have a higher emerald gem drop chance and a chance to drop superior gems

### drops

*   **:** Iron Ore 69.06%
*   **:** Uncut Emerald
    *   Grades:
        *   Low .5%
        *   Medium .4%
        *   High .3%
        *   superior .15%
