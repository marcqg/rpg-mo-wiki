This is the first world you step foot in once finishing the [Tutorial Island](/tutorial-island) quest. In this world, named after the main dockside city, we have the Paladin's Castle in the center and Minotaur Ruins to the North. Below ground is the [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon), [Dungeon II](/dungeon-ii), [Dungeon III](/dungeon-iii) and [Dungeon IV](/dungeon-iv) filled with ores and enemies, including the Rat Cave, Skeleton Cave and [Mining Guild](/mining-guild-permission). Entrances (Ladder) are scattered about the map with the Rat Cave being north-west of the blacksmith.

In this map, you will also find [Traveler's Signs](/travelers-sign) that can bring you to the neighboring towns of [Moche I](/moche-i) (West center) [Reval](/reval) (North West corner), [Whiland](/whiland) (South East corner), and [Walco](/walco) (North East corner).

This map was revamped in 2023 during the Halloween Event (21/10/23), with the [Clouds](/clouds) entrance being moved to right inside [Lost Woods](/lost-woods), paths simplified, and many new decorations around the Dorpat Town. Also, you can kill barrels.
