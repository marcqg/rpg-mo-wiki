The Fishing Guild is located on [Fellin Island](/fellin-island). To enter requires an equipped [Fishing Guild Permission](/fishing-guild-permission). To equip the permission requires level 80 Fishing skill.

[![Fishing Guild](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/61/Fishing_Guild.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/678?cb=20171225025125)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/61/Fishing_Guild.png/revision/latest?cb=20171225025125)

  

Fishing spots include

*   [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) [![Fishing Rod](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c7/Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224190356)](/fishing-rod)Level 1
*   [Fishing Net](/fishing-net) [![Fishing Net](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a1/Fishing_Net.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225000205)](/fishing-net)Levels 5 & 23
*   [Cage](/cage) [![Cage](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/b/b2/Cage.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225000336)](/cage)Level 35
*   [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon) [![Wooden Harpoon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/20/Wooden_Harpoon.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225000252)](/wooden-harpoon)Levels 50 & 58
*   [Iron Fishing Rod](/iron-fishing-rod)[![Iron Fishing Rod](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ca/Iron_Fishing_Rod.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226014630)](/iron-fishing-rod)Level 65

There are also two [Steel Harpoon](/steel-harpoon) [![Steel Harpoon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/43/Steel_Harpoon.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225000314)](/steel-harpoon) fishing spots, Levels 63 & 75, on [Fellin Island](/fellin-island) at the north west corner of the island and a Steel Harpoon spot, Level 63, at the north east corner. Cage Level 36, Fishing Rod Level 1 and Fishing Net Level 5 along the west side of island.
