MOS is the premium currency of RPG MO and is purchased with real money for in-game benefits. You are able to trade MOS purchased items to other players. Check [MOS Market](/mos-market) for a list.

## Alternative Game play Acquisition

There are a variety of ways to acquire MOS other than purchasing.

*   Complete Kill [Quests](/quests) or [Kill Dungeons](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) or [Skill Quests](/skill-quests)
*   Win a [Mini Game](/mini-games)
*   [Daily Rewards](/daily-rewards)
*   Write reviews to game review websites as outlined in the official forums, [here](http://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=8&t=3870).
*   Come up with original content (artwork primarily) that the devs approve and use.
