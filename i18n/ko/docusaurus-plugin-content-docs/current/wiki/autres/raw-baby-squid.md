**Raw Baby Squid** is an uncooked food item. It can be caught at a level 5 Fishing Net Spot by a player with level 5 [Fishing](/fishing). These fishing spots can be found in [Dorpat](/dorpat), [Fellin Island](/fellin-island), the Illusion Guild, [Rakblood](/rakblood), and [Reval](/reval). Catching a baby squid rewards 8 Fishing [experience](/experience).

A player with level 5 [Cooking](/cooking) can cook it into a [Cooked Baby Squid](/cooked-baby-squid) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) with an initial success rate of 50%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping at level 55). Successfully cooking a baby squid rewards 15 Cooking experience.
