**General Information:**

A Cut Sapphire can be obtained from using a [Chisel](/chisel) with an [Uncut Sapphire](/uncut-sapphire).

The minimum the level required to cut a Sapphire is level 10.

Each successful cut earns 20 Jewelry Skill exp.
