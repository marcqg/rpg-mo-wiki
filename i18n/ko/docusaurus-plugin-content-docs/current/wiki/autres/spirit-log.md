Spirit logs [![Spirit Log](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/0e/Spirit_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114174155)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/0e/Spirit_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114174155) require level 45 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) to obtain from a [Spirit Tree](/spirit-tree) using a [Steel Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Steel_Woodcutter%27s_Axe), granting 30 exp per log. They are used in [carpentry](/carpentry) at level 55, which gives 18 exp. Spirit logs are also used in the creation of the [player ship](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).
