The [Rakblood](/rakblood) Alchemist buys and sells various items used for and in the [alchemy](/alchemy) skill. He is located in the city of Rakblood with coordinates of (34,72). [Rakblood](/rakblood) can be accessed by using the ladder located in the lower center of [Whiland](/whiland), or by using the upper middle [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in [Narwa](/narwa).
