Fungiculture is the [skill](/skills) regarding growing mushrooms. The skill was added to the game with the [2021 anniversary event](https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=11105) in July, 2021.

## Guide

Fungiculture [experience](/experience) can come from growing mushrooms, crushing mushrooms with a [mortar and pestle](/mortar-and-pestle) to make mushroom spores, [captcha](/captcha), and a tiny bit from watering sawdust.

### Crushing mushrooms

Russula and dark mushrooms are the only mushrooms that can be bought directly from an NPC (in Whiland). Crushing mushrooms can sometimes yield two mushroom spores.

Fungiculture is very similar to [Farming](/farming), where instead of placing seeds on soil, mushrooms spores are placed on sawdust plots. To grow mushrooms with the Fungiculture [skill](/skills), you need access to your [Player Island](/player-island)'s dungeon. The dungeon ladder uses 200 fir logs, 200 oak logs, and 200 maple logs and requires level 30 [Carpentry](/carpentry). Mushroom spores come from NPCs (up to morel mushroom spores) and [Mob](/monsters) drops.

#### Sawdust

Sawdust craft window

Sawdust uses 30 willow logs and 45 oak logs, and requires level 30 [Carpentry](/carpentry). Sawdusts can only be crafted and placed while in the dungeon, not on the surface level of a [Player Island](/player-island). Once the sawdust is placed, it needs to be watered with a [watering can](/watering-can) or [multi-tool](/multi-tool) before you can plant mushroom spores on it.

#### Watering

Many players find it convenient to have a water tank in their dungeon for Fungiculture. There are three types of water tanks: bronze (100 units of water), iron (150 units), and gilded (300 units). To add water to a water tank, a player's [watering can](/watering-can) or [multi-tool](/multi-tool) needs to be full of water, otherwise using the water tank will attempt to take water from it. Water tanks can be moved without the water emptying, but removing it back into the player chest does empty it of its water. How to quickly fill a water tank: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ler2aQK8e2A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ler2aQK8e2A)

Bobdylan's site has a table of mushrooms/spores, their required levels, which NPCs you can buy and sell them at, and which maps the mushroom mobs are on: [https://www.rpgmobob.com/fungiculture](https://www.rpgmobob.com/fungiculture)

Example of a dungeon being used for fungiculture

The dungeon ladder uses 200 fir logs, 200 oak logs, and 200 maple logs and requires level 30 [Carpentry](/carpentry). A player's dungeon starts out as 5x5 tiles and can be upgraded with [MOS](/mos) or [coins](/coins). Dungeons can't be used for [Farming](/farming) or [Breeding](/breeding), but it can be used for storage in cabinets and [Fletching](/fletching). [Dungeon Themes](/dungeon-themes) can also be bought with MOS or coins.
