**Ruby Horn** is a dragon horn that can be used to craft various equipment or various potions if ground into dust. A player with 70 [Alchemy](/alchemy) can grind it to [Ruby Dust](/ruby-dust) using a [Mortar And Pestle](/mortar-and-pestle) with a success rate of 50%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Alchemy level. Successfully grinding the dust grants 65 Alchemy [experience](/experience).
