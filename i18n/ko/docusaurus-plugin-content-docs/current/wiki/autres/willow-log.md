[![Willow Log](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/ff/Willow_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114174132)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/ff/Willow_Log.png/revision/latest?cb=20230114174132)**Willow Log**  
Requires level 20 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) to obtain from a [Willow Tree](/willow-tree) using a [Woodcutter's Axe](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Axe), granting 18 exp per log. Willow logs are also used in [carpentry](/carpentry) at level 30, and in the construction of the [Player ship](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).
