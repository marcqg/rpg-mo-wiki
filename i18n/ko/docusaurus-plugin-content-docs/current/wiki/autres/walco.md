Walco is one of the many [Locations](/locations) (aka Zones) within the RPG MO world. The entrance to Walco is located in the North East of [Dorpat](/dorpat). At the North East of Walco, you can access [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle).
