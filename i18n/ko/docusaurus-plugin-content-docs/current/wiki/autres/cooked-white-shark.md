**Cooked White Shark** is a cooked food item that heals 16 [Health](/health). A player with level 60 [Cooking](/cooking) can cook a [Raw White Shark](/raw-white-shark) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) with a success rate of 35%; the success rate can be increased to 100% with an increased cooking level (capping out at level 125 Cooking). Successfully cooking a Raw White Shark grants 146 Cooking [experience](/experience).
