The Fire Tower is located in [Void I](/void-i). It is the hardest of the three towers, [Nature Tower](/nature-tower) being the easiest and [Ice Tower](/ice-tower) being medium difficulty, Completing each room gains you from 1000 to 3500 experience points towards your [Fighting Mode](/fighting-mode) stance. If you use arrows to kill the mob, [Archery](/archery) skill gets the bonus. There are 88 (Elite) mob rooms and [Gate](/gate) is in room 89. Like [Cathedral](/cathedral), all of your items are safe when you die and you exit outside Fire Tower with full health.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding the [Gate](/gate) is 686,700. Without using [Magic](/magic) and assuming a [combat stance](/fighting-mode) other than [Cordial](/fighting-mode), finishing the Fire Tower will grant you a total of 2,384,100 combat experience with 1,589,400 for your current [combat stance](/fighting-mode) and 794,700 for [Health](/health). This includes the total bonus experience of 216,000 which comes from dealing the final hit to each mob. Using the [Cordial](/fighting-mode) combat stance will give 2/3 of the total experience normally gained with all of the experience going to [Health](/health). Using [Magic](/magic) will change the mob experience due to how [Magic](/magic) experience is calculated.

The bonus experience per room is 1,000 for rooms 1 through 16; 2,500 for rooms 17 through 68; and 3,500 for rooms 69 through 88.
