The Woodcutter's Guild is Located in [Moche I](/moche-i) and is accessed using the [Woodcutter's Guild Permission](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Woodcutter%27s_Guild_Permission) and having woodcutting skill level of 80 or more. The Traveler's Sign on the right is from [Dorpat](/dorpat) located at the [Gray Wizards](/gray-wizard).
