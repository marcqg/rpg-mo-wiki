Combat Level, also known as the player's level, or CL cl.

To view your CL either type the command in chat /combats and look for your name and level amongst the other players on the world you are on, or if you have [/mods](/mods) enabled, type the command in chat /cl or mouse over your character for pop-up info.

Combat Level is used for :

*   Creating and joining [Party Quest Dungeons (PQs)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons).
*   For [PvP in NML](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/No_Man%27s_Land_(PVP)) to determine who can attack you.

*   **Aggressive monsters won't initiate an attack if they are 20 or more levels below the player's combat level (except in [Party Quest Dungeons](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) and [NML](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/No_Man%27s_Land)).**

It is a fairly bad way to determine one's fighting potential, Combat Level is represented by one's total base stats, since characters that specialize in one or two combat stats tend to be more powerful than those with balanced levels in all six skills. This is because [equipment](/equipment) stat values rise exponentially with increase of level required to wear them, thus better [equipment](/equipment) can be worn at lower levels.

If **(Acc+Str+Def+Health+Arch)** / 4 > Magic, then Magic divider = 6, else divider = 4

If **(Acc+Str+Def+Health+Magic)** / 4 > Archery, then Archery divider = 6, else divider = 4

Combat Level = **(Acc+Str+Def+Health)** / 4 + (Magic / Magic divider) + (Arch / archery divider)

A better way to determine one's overall fighting capability is:

*   compare total adjusted stats_(top right)_ with those of a mob.

This can be accomplished via mouse over, or on touchscreens you may poke the square the mob is on through a menu (such as inventory).

## Skill Level

Skills do not have a maximum level, however the observed soft cap is level 150 because it takes a long time to achieve this goal and skills do not currently have content released for beyond 150.

Temporary effects that increase or decrease skill levels are negated at 1 point per minute. Potions that increase skill levels and damage to health are the primary examples of this.
