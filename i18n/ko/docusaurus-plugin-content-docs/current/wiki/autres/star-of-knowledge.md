The Star of Knowledge is the center of magical studies. It is accessible from the North-East of [Blood River](/blood-river) (90,80). In the center of the map is an outpost with a furnace, a well, an anvil and a chest. To get in the town you should use the Illusion Gate (80,66) near the entry (91,66), but be careful with the skeletons (290 and 350 ACC) on the way!

One of the main features are the Illusion gates, which provide one-way transportation to the spellcrafting altars. To return to the Star Of Knowledge, one can travel through Blood River or use a Star Of Knowledge Teleport which is sold by the Star Of Knowledge Magician for 25,000 coins.

Check also all the [Locations](/locations).

## **Parts of the Star of Knowledge**

*   The Outpost in the center with the Five Tongues of Elements where you can access each Altar.
*   The Hellish Swamp in the East, South-East.
*   The Cloudy Necropolis in the North-East.
*   The Desert Canyon in the Noth-West.
*   The Windy Marsh in the West, South-West.
*   The Icy Hag-Forest in the South.

_Nota bene_: If you want to kill the mobs, you can only start it in the Hellish Swamp. Then, you can choose two ways to advance: 1) If you turn left you can proceed counter-clockwise passing by the Illusion Gates which lead you through the parts of the Star of Knowledge. 2) If you turn right, you can proceed clockwise fighting through the dangerous Werewolves.

## **Illusion Gates within the Star of Knowledge**

*   Entrance to the [Air Altar](/spell-crafting) (61,80)
*   Entrance to the [Earth Altar](/spell-crafting) (24,41)
*   Entrance to the [Fire Altar](/spell-crafting) (73,54)
*   Entrance to the [Flash Altar](/spell-crafting) (23,78)
*   Entrance to the [Water Altar](/spell-crafting) (49,27)
