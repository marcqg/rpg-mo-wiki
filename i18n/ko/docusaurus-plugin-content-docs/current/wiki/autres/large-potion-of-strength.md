A **Large Potion Of Strength** is a potion made from a [Large Vial Of Water](/large-vial-of-water) and four [Reptile Eyes](/reptile-eye). A player with 63 [Alchemy](/alchemy) has a 30% chance to successfully craft the potion; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 60% with an increased Alchemy level. Successfully crafting this potion will grant 95 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

There is a 5% chance that a Large Potion of Strength (Rare) is made instead of this potion.
