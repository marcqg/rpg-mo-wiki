"With [Jewelry](/jewelry) you can make moulds, rings and necklaces. to do jewelry you need to mine materials. First you have to start mining clay to make ring moulds. Equip clay and click on a [Furnace](/furnace) to smelt it into a ring mould. you need 3 clay to make 1 ring mould. you can later combine a rig mould with iron, silver and other minerals and gems to produce valuable items. As you get higher jewelry levels you can make better things and use more materials."
