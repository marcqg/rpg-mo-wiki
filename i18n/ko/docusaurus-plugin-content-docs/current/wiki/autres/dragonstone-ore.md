## Use

At lvl 80 Forging you can combine 8x **Dragonstone Ores** at a Furnace to make [Dragonstone Bar](/dragonstone-bar).

## Mining

Requires lvl 110 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [No Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/No_Man%27s_Land),[Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle),[Dungeon III](/dungeon-iii).
