1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw FishA **Raw Eel** is a type of fish that can be caught at [Fishing](/fishing) level 47 with a [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) giving 46 fishing experience. It can be cooked into an [Eel](/eel) at [Cooking](/cooking) level 47 giving 76 cooking experience. When cooked and eaten, Eel heals 12 [Health](/health).
