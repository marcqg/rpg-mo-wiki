Rakblood contains several monsters of varying levels, as well as locations for mining and fishing. The map consists of three primary areas which include, the Golem Maze, City island, and Grass lands with conjoining snowy right corner. The city of Rakblood can be found on the middle of the center brown and green island and is surrounded by water and a stone wall; access to the city is made by crossing a single wooden bridge. When arriving in Rakblood from [Whiland](/whiland), the player will encounter two [Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) level 26 which cannot be avoided when traveling to the City of Rakblood; except when traveling to [Narwa](/narwa), all other monsters can be avoided with enough [Combat Level](/combat-level). Rakblood is the home of the starfish.

## **Entrances**

Other than using a Scroll or potion, Rakblood can be entered on foot by either using the...

*   Ladder (down) located in [Whiland](/whiland) (71,46) arrives in Rakblood (39,21).
*   Ladder (up) located in [Narwa](/narwa) (19, 81) arrives in Rakblood (87, 91)

Dangers:

Access to Rakblood Town from [Whiland](/whiland) is blocked 2 [Combat Level](/combat-level) 26 [Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) (directly, more if [Aggro](/combat-level))

Access to [Narwa](/narwa) from Rakblood is blocked by 3 [Combat Level](/combat-level) 92 [Rock Spirits](/rock-spirit) ([Aggro](/combat-level)).

## **Quests**

For the 10th [kill quest](/quests) "Mining Some Golems Part 1" the recommended location to kill [Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) is Rakblood.

For the 13th [kill quest](/quests) "[Explore Explorers](/explore-explorers)", the recommended location to kill [Explorers](/explorer) is Rakblood.

For the 17th [kill quest](/quests) "Mining some Golems Part 2" the recommended location to kill [Iron Golems](/iron-golem) is Rakblood.

## **Mining**

Rakblood is an excellent place to train the [Mining](/mining) skill. Within the Golem Maze can be found [Tin](/tin), [Iron](/iron-ore), [Coal](/coal) and [White Gold](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/White_Gold). When storing ores in the city chest, the player must kill at least 3 [Bronze Golem](/bronze-golem) when traveling to and from [Iron Ore](/iron-ore) or [White Gold](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/White_Gold), and 2 [Bronze Golems](/bronze-golem) when mining [Iron](/iron-ore) or [Coal](/coal). If mining [Iron](/iron-ore) without combat is the players sole desire (as it saves time to continue mining), it is recommended to mine [Iron](/iron-ore) in [Dorpat](/dorpat) [Skeleton Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) and store ores in the [Dorpat Outpost](/dorpat) [Chest](/chest).
