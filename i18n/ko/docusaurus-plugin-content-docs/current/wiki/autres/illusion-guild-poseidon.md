> 🇰🇷 한국어 버전 — <a href="/rpg-mo-wiki/illusion-guild-poseidon">see the English page</a>

This special location is not on any map and may only be reached using [Poseidon's Permission](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Permission) obtained through the [Market](/market-guide) from another player or by catching it using [Poseidon's Trident](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Trident) at those fishing spots.

Using [Poseidon's Permission](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Permission) teleports you to the guild for **one hour** and includes a [Chest](/chest), a [Campfire](/cooking) and many fishing tool locations. You do receive a bonus to catch rates in the guild but [Poseidon's Permission](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Permission) is not available to be caught there. When the timer runs out, you will be teleported to your original starting location and can use another permission to reenter the Illusion Guild for another hour.

Be careful not to click on the exit sign when moving in the guild or you will end your time early.

You can change worlds while in the guild and will remain inside. If you used a potion before switching, the potion icon will not be visible but your bonus will still be active. Maintenance locks you out of your chest 2 minutes before it happens so switching is an option to deposit and keep fishing.

See Fishing (New) for a full listing of fish catches and other information.
