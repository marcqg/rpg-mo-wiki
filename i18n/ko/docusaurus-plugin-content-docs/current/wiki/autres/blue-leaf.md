Blue Leaf can be grown from[Blue Leaf Seed](/blue-leaf-seed) with level 45 [Farming](/farming). It is the only ingredient (other than a [Medium Vial Of Water](/medium-vial-of-water)) for a [Medium Potion of Strength](/medium-potion-of-strength).

## Obtained from Monsters

*   [Wind Elemental](/wind-elemental)
*   [Water Elemental](/water-elemental)
*   [Ice Troglodyte](/ice-troglodyte)
*   [Ice Elemental](/ice-elemental)
*   [Fire Viper](/fire-viper)
*   [Earth Elemental](/earth-elemental)
