## Use

At lvl 40 [Jewelry](/jewelry) you can combine 3x **White Gold Chunks** at a [Furnace](/furnace) to make White Gold Bar.

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 55 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Clouds](/clouds),[Heaven](/heaven),[Rakblood](/rakblood).

Fastest way is mining from [Heaven](/heaven) but you have to kill 2 [Grendalf The Greys](/grendalf-the-grey).

For low level players Rakblood is the best because only a [Bronze Golem](/bronze-golem) blocks your path.
