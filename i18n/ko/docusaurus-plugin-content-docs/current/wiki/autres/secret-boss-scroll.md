The Secret Boss Scroll [![Secret Boss Scroll](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a3/Secret_Boss_Scroll.png/revision/latest?cb=20170921001855)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a3/Secret_Boss_Scroll.png/revision/latest?cb=20170921001855)is an item that only drops from [Cow King](/cow-king) mob In [Every Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Every_Man%27s_Land) and only when that land is open: i.e. [Events](/events) or second week of a month without an event. It is used like a [Teleport Book](/teleport-scrolls) in that you choose the destination (Boss Mob) you want to fight inside the cage within [Every Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Every_Man%27s_Land). As the Danger Level is above 0 (it is over 50) you can only change worlds through logging out to fight more than one boss, they respawn like any other boss in the game.

The Bosses are:

1.  Cannibal Plant
2.  Nephilim Warrior
3.  Ancient Hydra
