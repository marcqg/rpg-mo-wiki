You can enter it by going to the east side of [Walco](/walco), though the illusion gate. Take the south portal 4 times and you will enter the forest. You can also access [Wittensten](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Wittenstein) here. There is a chest, so its possible to use the market. In the southern part of the map is the entrance to the dungeon [Cathedral](/cathedral), which is available every 48 hours.
