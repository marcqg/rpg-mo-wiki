Template:Infobox Item NewFirelord Boots can be acquired by buying them for 150 MOS from [MOS Market](/mos-market) or through the [Chest Market](/market-guide) from other players. They are also part of the [Firelord Set](/firelord-set) at 500 MOS. They require Defense to equip. They can be enchanted many times using Armor [Enchant Scrolls](/enchanting) to add more Armor and Speed plus Power and Aim at +7 however this also increases the Defense needed to equip them. Be sure you can equip the next level Boots before you enchant the ones you have. [Enchanting](/enchanting) is done at the [Anvil](/anvil). [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)
