Template:Infobox Item Cotton Fabric is a material used in crafting Magic Armor, [Spells](/spell-crafting) and Archery Bows. It is also used in making your own [Ship](/ship-building). [Cotton Seeds](/cotton-seed) can be purchased from the [Dorpat Farmer](/dorpat-farmer) for 240 coins. Cotton Fabric can be purchased from the [Dorpat Magic Crafter](/dorpat-magic-crafter) for 300 coins.

Here is a list of items you can craft using Cotton Fabric in your inventory. Only the fabric and thread is used during the crafting. NOTE: Some of the crafted items are also Loot Drops from Mobs.
