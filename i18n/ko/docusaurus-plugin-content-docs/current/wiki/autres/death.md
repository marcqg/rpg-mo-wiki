Death will happen when a player's [Health](/health) reaches zero. After death a player will respawn in [Dorpat](/dorpat) and lose everything in their inventory **Except**:

*   Their 2 most expensive items (by NPC price, a.k.a "wiki value").
*   Their equipped pet.
*   Anything their pet is holding.
*   The pet will lose half of its current exp.

[![Nw 2019-11-05 11-44-26](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e6/Nw_2019-11-05_11-44-26.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/217?cb=20191105034727)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e6/Nw_2019-11-05_11-44-26.png/revision/latest?cb=20191105034727)

Lost items are gone and you must replace them on your own.

NOTE: If [Mods](/mods) are enabled, a small green dot will be present in the corner of the inventory item that will be saved.

## Exceptions to Standard Death Consequences

*   Players have a 24 hour grace period after first login in which they will not lose most of their items upon death.
*   The mini game [Arena](/arena) is a specialized combat zone. It has its own items separate from your own and you do not get teleported to Dorpat.
*   [Cathedral](/cathedral) does not have death consequences. Upon death you do not lose any items and are teleported to the entrance of the Cathedral with full life.
*   [Towers](/tower) do not have death consequences. Upon death you do not lose any items and are teleported to the entrance of the tower you were in with full life.

*   Dueling another player, upon death, will teleport you to [Dorpat](/dorpat) but you do not lose any items unless you stake them as part of the duel.
    
    [![Potion of preservation](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2f/Potion_of_preservation.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/413?cb=20191105033234)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2f/Potion_of_preservation.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20191105033234)
    
*   An equipped [Potion of Preservation](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_of_Preservation) saves five additional items upon death. Total of seven items.
