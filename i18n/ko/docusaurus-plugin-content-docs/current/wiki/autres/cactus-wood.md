[![Cactus Wood](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/54/Cactus_Wood.png/revision/latest?cb=20230103155426)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/54/Cactus_Wood.png/revision/latest?cb=20230103155426)**Cactus Wood**  
Requires level 5 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) to obtain from a [Cactus](/cactus), which can be found in [Reval](/reval) and [Dorpat](/dorpat).

Successfully cutting a cactus will grant 8 exp per log.

Cactus wood is also used in [Carpentry](/carpentry) at level 10.

150 cactus wood is also required for the construction of the [player ship](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).

Can be purchased by the [Reval Shopkeeper](/reval-shopkeeper) for 30 coins per piece.
