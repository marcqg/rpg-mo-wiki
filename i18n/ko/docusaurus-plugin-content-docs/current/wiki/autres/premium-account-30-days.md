## 500 MOS - Provides the buyer with the following:

Access to US based server - faster connection using World 3.

Fewer players in premium world - less competition to kill bosses or other mobs.

[Daily Login Reward](/daily-rewards) won't be reset when you miss a day - Your next login continues your daily count.

Write chat in light blue text instead of white.

Create your own private chat channel - channel continues to exist after premium expires.

Market offers last 48 hours instead of 24 hours.

3 extra equipment preset slots accessed from Equipment window.

Monster island extra 1 boss monster per day.

Lower market tax (0.1% less for each month down to 3% vs 4% normal).

An extra island! (added 04/06/23)

Binding "On Screen Buttons" to key good one are send command /join, /timer, /cooldowns or simply withdraw forging mats.

Max Contacts from 300 to 600

6 month /market history instead of 1 week

Ability to customize attack animations

An achievement for using premium.

Support the Game! ;)

## Premium Scroll - 600 MOS

Provides a scroll you can sell on the Player Market with above benefits

## Duration

Premium or Premium Scroll can be used immediately to give you 30 days of Premium benefits. You can use a second to continue your Premium status after the first day giving you a total max of 59 days. Use the chat command /premium to check your time remaining or buy more time at 500 MOS. Use the MOS Market to buy the scroll.
