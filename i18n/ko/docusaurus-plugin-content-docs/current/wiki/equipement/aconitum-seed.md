**Aconitum Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 65 [Farming](/farming) and produces [Aconitum](/aconitum) after 17 minutes. Harvesting the plant grants 70 Farming [experience](/experience). These seeds can be purchased from the [Wandering Farmer](/wandering-farmer) in [Whiland](/whiland) or obtained while cutting [Blue Palm Logs](/blue-palm-log). The chance of getting a seed while woodcutting is 3% and grants 45 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) experience.
