## Craftable

Smelt 2 Sand [![Sand-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/50/Sand-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211251)](/sand) at a [Furnace](/furnace) [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Alchemy](/alchemy) level 50, Min chance 20% - Max 30% - 20 exp.

Combine 3 [Empty Medium Vials](/empty-medium-vial)[![Empty Medium Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c0/Empty_Medium_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155338)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c0/Empty_Medium_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155338) at a [Furnace](/furnace)[![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Forging](/forging) level 1, 100% chance - 3 exp.

Fill with water from a town [Water Well](/water-well)[![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601) to create a [Large Vial of Water](/large-vial-of-water)[![Large Vial Of Water](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/b/b1/Large_Vial_Of_Water.png/revision/latest?cb=20210304052458)](/large-vial-of-water)
