**Air Blast** is a level 50 [Magic](/magic) spell scroll; in order to be used against mobs, the scroll needs to be loaded into an equipped pouch. Each scroll will add 60 casts of Air Blast. The spell has a cooldown of 9 seconds and a maximum hit of 25. Each cast will grant 58 Magic [experience](/experience) regardless of the amount of damage dealt.

Scrolls can be crafted by a player with level 60 [Wizardry](/wizardry) using an Air Blast Medallion and a [Jute Fabric](/jute-fabric) at an Air Altar with a success rate of 50%; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Wizardry level (capping at level 110 Wizardry). Successfully crafting a scroll grants 75 Wizardry experience.
