1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw Fish**Raw Red Star** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Net](/fishing-net), and can be cooked into a [Red Star](/red-star). The Raw Red Star cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Rock Centipede](/rock-centipede) (5%)
*   [Skeleton King](/skeleton-king) (3%)
