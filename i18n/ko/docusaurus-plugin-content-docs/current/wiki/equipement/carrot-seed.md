**Carrot Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 48 [Farming](/farming) and produces a Carrot after 13 minutes. Harvesting the vegetable grants 50 Farming [experience](/experience).
