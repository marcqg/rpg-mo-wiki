## Craftable

## Can be Obtained From

Reclaim [Empty Medium Vial](/empty-medium-vial)[![Empty Medium Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/c0/Empty_Medium_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155338)](/empty-medium-vial) after drinking most medium potions.

## Uses

Equip it with an ingredient in your inventory to craft Medium sized potions. Requires more than one of a particular ingredient to make a potion and it can fail giving no exp for the attempt. See [Alchemy](/alchemy) for more.

Also used in making [Soil](/soil) for your [Island](/island-deed).
