**Iron Full Helmet** is a helmet that requires 20 [Defense](/defense) to equip. A player with 28 [Forging](/forging) has an 85% chance of crafting a helmet with 5 [iron bars](/iron-bar) at an [anvil](/anvil) while having a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped. Successfully crafting a helmet will grant 100 Forging [experience](/experience).
