**Raw Frog** is one of the first fish available to catch. It can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon), [Steel Harpoon](/steel-harpoon) or [Poseidon's Trident](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon%27s_Trident). It can be cooked into a [Frog](/frog). The Raw Frog cannot be consumed.
