**Dorpat Soul** is a soul item that is used to make teleports to [Dorpat](/dorpat). It can be obtained by cutting [Fir Trees](/fir-tree) or as a mob drop.

## Alchemy

A [Memory of Dorpat](/memory-of-dorpat) can be crafted with a [Small Vial Of Water](/small-vial-of-water) and six Dorpat Souls. A player with level 24 [Alchemy](/alchemy) has a success rate of 50%; the success rate can be raised to a maximum of 80% with an increased Alchemy level. Successfully crafting the potion will grant 35 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

## Wizardry

A [Dorpat Teleport](/dorpat-teleport) can be crafted at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped and two [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) and six Dorpat Souls. A player needs level 24 [Wizardry](/wizardry) and there is a flat success rate of 60%. Successfully crafting the scroll will grant 34 Wizardry experience.
