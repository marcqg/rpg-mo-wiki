## Craftable

Smelt 2 Sand [![Sand-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/50/Sand-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211251)](/sand) at a [Furnace](/furnace) [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Alchemy](/alchemy) level 30, Min chance 30% - Max 40% - 15 exp.

Combine 3 [Empty Small Vials](/empty-small-vial)[![Empty Small Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/81/Empty_Small_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225162405)](/empty-small-vial) at a [Furnace](/furnace)[![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) at [Forging](/forging) level 1, 100% chance - 1 exp.

Fill with water from a town [Water Well](/water-well)[![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601) to create a [Medium Vial of Water](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Medium_Vial_of_Water)[![Medium Vial Of Water](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/4a/Medium_Vial_Of_Water.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215245)](/medium-vial-of-water)
