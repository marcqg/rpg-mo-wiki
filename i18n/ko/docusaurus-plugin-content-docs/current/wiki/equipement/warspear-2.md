Template:Infobox Item NewEnchanted from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) item [Boar Spear](/boar-spear), Warspear +2 is the second level weapon in the series. This weapon requires Strength to equip and it can be enchanted many times using Weapon Enchant Scrolls to get more Aim and Power however this also increases the Strength needed to equip it. Be sure you can wield the next level Spear before you enchant the one you have. [Enchanting](/enchanting) is done at the [Anvil](/anvil).
