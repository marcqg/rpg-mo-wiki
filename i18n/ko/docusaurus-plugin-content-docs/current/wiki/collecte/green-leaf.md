> 🇰🇷 한국어 버전 — <a href="/rpg-mo-wiki/green-leaf">see the English page</a>

Acquire by growing [Green Leaf Seed](/green-leaf-seed) on your [Island](/player-island) at level 1 [Farming](/farming) or cutting [Oak Tree](/oak-tree) at level 10 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting).

Use two Green Leaf(s) and one [Small Vial of Water](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Small_Vial_of_Water) in your [Inventory](/inventory) to craft [Small Potion of Accuracy](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Small_Potion_of_Accuracy) at 50% to 70% chance starting at level 1 [Alchemy](/alchemy).
