The Blue Palm Tree can be cut at level 55 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting). When cut down successfully, a [Blue Palm Log](/blue-palm-log) is acquired. The value of the log is 350 coins. The log can be used in skills such as carpentry and forging. Template:Infobox Tree
