**Tin Ore** is an ore harvested from a tin vein, or can be bought from the [Dorpat Blacksmith](/dorpat-blacksmith). It's used in conjunction with [copper ore](/copper) in order to create [bronze bars](/bronze-bar). Template:Infobox Item

## Can be Obtained From

*   Tin Vein
*   [Dorpat Blacksmith](/dorpat-blacksmith)
*   [Bronze Golem](/bronze-golem)
