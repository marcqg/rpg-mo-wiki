## Use

At lvl 40 [Forging](/forging) you can combine at a [Furnace](/furnace) with [Iron Ore](/iron-ore) to make [Steel Bar](/steel-bar).

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 40 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from: [Mining Guild](/mining-guild), [Reval](/reval), [Narwa](/narwa), and [Rakblood](/rakblood).

Fastest way is mining from [Mining Guild](/mining-guild) and using the [chest](/chest) in [Dorpat](/dorpat).

To get to the coal in [Reval](/reval), as soon as you get there from Dorpat, go North: you'll have to navigate through a large field of [Desert Orcs](/desert-orc). You can dodge every encounter on your way, including some [Fire Imps](/fire-imp), and you'll get to the Coal.

Use the command /savemap to get an overall look of the Reval (Area4).
