ON STANDBY TO VERIFY % RATES AND MOB DROPS

Medium Enchanting Scrolls [![Medium Weapon Enchant Scroll](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/46/Medium_Weapon_Enchant_Scroll.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202013026)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/46/Medium_Weapon_Enchant_Scroll.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202013026) allow for the Enchanting of [Weapons](/weapons), [Armor](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Armor), [Jewelry](/jewelry) and [Wings](/wings) with a 25% [success chance](/success-chance). Enchanting is done at the [Anvil](/anvil) [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015) using the [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) [![Forging Hammer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a1/Forging_Hammer.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224204643)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a1/Forging_Hammer.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224204643) by placing the Scroll and the item in the [Forging](/forging) grid and selecting 'Make'. Adding the Orb Of Luck [![Orb Of Luck](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/ad/Orb_Of_Luck.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232512)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/ad/Orb_Of_Luck.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225232512) adds 10% to [success chance](/success-chance). See [Enchanting](/enchanting) for more information.
