Ores are the resource obtained by [Mining](/mining). Ores are a great way of earning Coin and are used as a base material for [Forging](/forging), and the Gems are primarily used in [Jewelry](/jewelry). The maximum chances for all ores are 100% minus the gem chances and for gems the [success chances](/success-chance) are fixed. Every +1 level above the required one, the success chances for ores increase by +1%.

For the best exp a list has been left below:

*   1-25 tin/copper (avg from tin/copper at lv25 is 5.5)
*   25-40 silver (avg from silver at lv40 is 10 vs. 9 from iron) \*mine silver to lv45 if you want to skip coal\*
*   40-45 coal (avg from coal is higher at lv45 than gold by .25xp, it quickly be lower than gold)
*   45-55 gold (avg from gold at lv55 is 16 vs. 17.5 from white gold)
*   55-125 white gold (avg from white gold at lv80 is 30 vs 24.75 from azure, 18.75 from platinum, and 12.75 from fire stone)
*   125-140 azure
*   140-265 fire stone
*   265+ dragonstone

Note: This list does not take into account the storing of ores. To achieve the maximum potential from this list you must power-mine. All information is based on what is available on this wiki page.
