Mining is the gathering [skill](/skills) upon which the skills [Alchemy](/alchemy), [Jewelry](/jewelry) and [Forging](/forging) are founded. Ores are mined from veins which can be smelted at a furnace to create crafting materials. If you have the ores, the level necessary to use them and the proper materials for the item you are attempting to make, you can smelt them into next level ingredients.

To smelt ores that you have mined you must equip them and use them at a furnace: [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace)

See [Forging](/forging) for how to process the ores. You can also get gems when mining.

## Mining Guild

Located near [Dorpat Town](/dorpat) is the Ladder down to the [Dungeon I](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) sub-zone known as the [Mining Guild](/mining-guild). To enter the [Mining Guild](/mining-guild) requires having a [Mining Guild Permission](/mining-guild-permission) (Lv65 **Mining**) equipped. This permit can be bought in [Cesis](/cesis) or [Hell](/hell) from the Blacksmith (NPC) for 98,000 Coins.

## Powerleveling

This list assumes the player is attempting to get the highest xp/hr for Mining by destroying the gems/ores, so any time dedicated to depositing materials is not considered.

*   [Clay](/clay): Levels 1 - 25
*   [Silver Chunk](/silver-chunk): Levels 26 - 39
*   [Coal](/coal): Levels 40 - 49
*   [Kaolinite Clay](/kaolinite-clay): Levels 50 - 90
*   Blood Crystal: Levels 91+

If you don't want to mine Blood Crystals in No Man's Land:

*   [Clay](/clay): Levels 1 - 25
*   [Silver Chunk](/silver-chunk): Levels 26 - 39
*   [Coal](/coal): Levels 40 - 49
*   [Kaolinite Clay](/kaolinite-clay): Levels 50 - 107 (or 113)
*   [Redhodium Chunk](/redhodium-chunk): Levels 108 - 125 (via Hades guild) or [Fire Stone Chunk](/fire-stone-chunk): Levels 114 - 125
*   [Edoylerite Ore](/edoylerite-ore): Levels 126+
