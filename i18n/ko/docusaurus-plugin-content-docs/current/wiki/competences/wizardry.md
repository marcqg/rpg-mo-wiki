Wizardry is the newest skill introduced to the world of RPG-MO and is the skill used to craft [Magic](/magic) equipment such as Pouches, Spell Scrolls, Hats, Robes, Robe Bottoms, Boots and Teleport Scrolls.

## Pouch Crafting

Pouches which hold your Spell Scrolls can be crafted mostly at the [Anvil](/anvil)[![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)and Rose Pouch in your [Inventory](/inventory). Most craft using Wizardry Skill except Rose Pouch uses Jewelry level 1 with crafting adding to Jewelry skill and [Santa's Bag](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Santa%27s_Bag) uses Forging level 1 but gives no exp when crafted.

## Gear Crafting

All materials are consumed in the attempt but you do get half exp on failure. See [Forging](/forging). All gear can be recycled for a 50% to recover base materials if you have the Wizardry skill to craft.
