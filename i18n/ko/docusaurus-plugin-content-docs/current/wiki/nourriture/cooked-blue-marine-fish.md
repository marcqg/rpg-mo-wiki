**Cooked Blue Marine Fish** is a type of food that is obtained by cooking [Raw Blue Marine Fish](/raw-blue-marine-fish) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) or as a mob drop. A player with 23 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 50% chance of succesfully cooking the fish; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level. Successfully cooking the fish grants 29 Cooking [experience](/experience). It heals 5 [health](/health) when eaten.
