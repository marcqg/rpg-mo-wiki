**Cooked Deep Sea Octopus** is a type of food that is obtained by cooking [Raw Deep Sea Octopus](/raw-deep-sea-octopus) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire). A player with 80 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 30% chance of successfully cooking the octopus; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level. Successfully cooking the octopus grants 200 Cooking [experience](/experience). They heal 19 [Health](/health) when eaten.
