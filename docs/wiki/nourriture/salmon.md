**Salmon** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Salmon](/raw-salmon) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 30 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

## Can be Obtained From

*   Cooking [Raw Salmon](/raw-salmon)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Kobalos](/kobalos) (10%)
*   [Black Rat](/black-rat) (10%)
