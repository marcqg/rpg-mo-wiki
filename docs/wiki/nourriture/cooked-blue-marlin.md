**Cooked Blue Marlin** is a type of food that is obtained by cooking [Raw Blue Marlin](/raw-blue-marlin) on a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire). A player with 108 [Cooking](/cooking) has a 20% chance of successfully cooking the fish; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Cooking level (capping at level 188). Successfully cooking the blue marlin grants 422 Cooking [experience](/experience). They heal 26 [Health](/health) when eaten.
