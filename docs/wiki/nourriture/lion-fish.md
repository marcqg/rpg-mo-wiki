**Lion Fish** can be obtained by cooking [Raw Lion Fish](/raw-lion-fish) on a Camp Fire. It requires Level 35 in [Cooking](/cooking) to do so.

*   Cooking [Raw Lion Fish](/raw-lion-fish)
*   [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Battlemage](/battlemage) (5%)
*   [Blood Battlemage](/blood-battlemage) (5%)
*   [Royal Griffin](/royal-griffin) (1%)
