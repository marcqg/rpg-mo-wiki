Other than the [Mining Guild](/mining-guild) at higher [Levels](/mining), the easiest place to mine [Iron Ore](/iron-ore) at low levels is the [Skeleton](/skeleton) [Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon), East-South-East of [Dorpat](/dorpat) Town, West of Dorpat Outpost.

[![Skeleton dungeon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/df/Skeleton_dungeon.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150706120545)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/df/Skeleton_dungeon.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20150706120545)

The **Iron** is surrounded by Lv36-44 [mobs](/monsters), however there is a path between the [mobs](/monsters) to get to the [Mining Nodes](/mining) without fighting. Be aware that there are two kinds of [Mining Nodes](/mining) that give [![Link=Iron Ore](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Iron_Ore.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211438)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Iron_Ore.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211438 "Link=Iron Ore") [Iron Ore](/iron-ore), they are [![Iron](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8f/Iron.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143655)](/iron) **Iron** and [![Iron Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/Iron_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003134512)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/IronVein) [Iron Vein](/iron-vein), the difference between them is that the **Iron** is mine-able by the [![Link=Iron Pickaxe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a2/Iron_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203458)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/a2/Iron_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150224203458 "Link=Iron Pickaxe") [Iron Pickaxe](/iron-pickaxe) whilst the [Iron Vein](/iron-vein) requires a [![Link=Steel Pickaxe](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d1/Steel_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225231857)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d1/Steel_Pickaxe.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225231857 "Link=Steel Pickaxe") [Steel Pickaxe](/steel-pickaxe).
