The _**Willow tree**_ can be cut at level 20 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) and above. When cut down successfully, a [Willow Log](/willow-log) is acquired. The standard listed value of a single unit of Willow logs is 120 coins. The log can be used in skills such as [Carpentry](/carpentry), [Forging](/forging), and is a necessary material for player-constructed [ships](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Player_ship).

### Location

Willows can be found in:

*   [Dorpat](/dorpat) - North East corner above Outpost, near Walco Traveller's Sign. Just north of the Golem Pit, right there by all the spiders
*   [Reval](/reval) - beside entrance to Cesis
