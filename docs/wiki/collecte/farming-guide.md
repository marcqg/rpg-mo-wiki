## Welcome to Farming

Farming in RPG.MO is not the easiest to start of with. But because you are here, you probably have heard that farming is very profitable if done right. With that said, welcome to farming.

## **Beginner's Guide**

To get started [Farming](/farming), First of all you need to buy:

*   [![Island Deed](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Island_Deed.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233723)](/island-deed) [Island Deed](/island-deed): 35,000 Coins
*   [![Rake](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/b/bd/Rake.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](/rake) [Rake](/rake): 240 Coins
*   [![Grass Seed](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/ec/Grass_Seed.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233805)](/grass-seed) [Grass Seed](/grass-seed): 2 Coins (1 Seed per Soil)

The easiest place to acquire these things is from the [![Dorpat Farmer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/0a/Dorpat_Farmer.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128222553)](/dorpat-farmer) [Dorpat Farmer](/dorpat-farmer). All Farmers sell [Island Deeds](/island-deed) and [Rakes](/rake), Different Farmers for different Seeds.

Now you need some [![Soil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/42/Soil.png/revision/latest?cb=20240210202541)](/soil) [Soil](/soil), other than purchasing it on the Player Market, you can Craft it using one of the following Recipes:

*   35 [Small Vials Of Water](/small-vial-of-water)
*   25 [Medium Vials Of Water](/medium-vial-of-water)
*   15 [Large Vials Of Water](/large-vial-of-water)
*   15 [Superior Vials Of Water](/superior-vial-of-water)

You can make Empty Vials by Smelting 2 [![Sand-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/50/Sand-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211251)](/sand) [Sand](/sand) that you mine from [Reval](/reval) or [Dorpat](/dorpat) at the [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) [Furnace](/furnace).

You can then fill the

at a [![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](/water-well) [Water Well](/water-well) to (respectively) create a:

## Watering Cans

Crops that haven't been harvested within a week will wither so you need to use a [Watering Can](/watering-can) to revive those dead crops. Each [Watering Can](/watering-can) holds a set number of water units and shares the water supply. Having more than one can is unnecessary. You can refill it at a well located in various towns. A [Watering Can](/watering-can) also needs a required farming level to use. Using a [Watering Can](/watering-can) will give 1 farming exp and reduce 1 minute from a plants grow time but it is negligible in the long run. You must unequip a [Watering Can](/watering-can) before you can harvest any crop.

Reviving brings each plant back to 'just planted' status to begin the growth timer.

*   Bronze Watering Can = 10 water units (level 1 farming)
*   Iron Watering Can = 15 water units (level 20 farming)
*   Steel Watering Can = 20 water units (level 40 farming)
*   Azurite Watering Can = 25 water units (level 50 farming)
*   Gilded Watering Can = 30 water units (level 55 farming)
