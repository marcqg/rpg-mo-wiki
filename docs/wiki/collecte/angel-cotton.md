**Angel Cotton** is an ingredient used in [Alchemy](/alchemy) at level 40 to craft [Angel Feathers](/angel-feather) and Angel Feather Bundles.

It can be obtained from [Farming](/farming) at level 40 using [Angel Tree Seeds](/angel-tree-seed).
