Located in [Dungeon I](/dungeon-i), the Ladder down to here can be reached from just South-East of [Dorpat](/dorpat) City, the **Mining Guild** requires the Player to have a [Mining Guild Permission](/mining-guild-permission) equipped (Lv65 [Mining](/mining)) to be granted access.

The **Mining Guild** contains the following Ore Deposits: [![Copper](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/47/Copper.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143010)](/copper) [Copper](/copper), [![Copper Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Copper_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003134511)](/copper) [Copper Ore](/copper), [![Tin](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/69/Tin.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143656)](/tin) [Tin](/tin), [![Tin Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/6e/Tin_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143008)](/tin) [Tin Ore](/tin), [![Iron](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8f/Iron.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143655)](/iron) [Iron](/iron), [![Iron Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/cc/Iron_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003134512)](/iron-vein) [Iron Vein](/iron-vein), [![Coal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/1/1e/Coal.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143656)](/coal) [Coal](/coal), and [![Coal Vein](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/39/Coal_Vein.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143009)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Coal_Vein) Coal Vein; along with a [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](/furnace) [Furnace](/furnace) and [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](/anvil) [Anvil](/anvil).

Due to the [Furnace](/furnace)'s proximity to the Ores and [Anvil](/anvil), it is a great place to quickly train [Forging](/forging) at lower levels by, for example; smelting the [Iron Ores](/iron-ore) into [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) at the [Furnace](/furnace) then [Forging](/forging) the [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) at the [Anvil](/anvil) into something that has a high chance of success (like [Iron Watering Can](/iron-watering-can)), recycling the creations and doing it all over again or selling the results to the various NPC [![Dorpat Blacksmith](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Blacksmith.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128222524)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/e/e4/Dorpat_Blacksmith.png/revision/latest?cb=20221128222524) vendors that buy such things.

This is also a better place to mine [![Coal-0](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/8c/Coal-0.png/revision/latest?cb=20151001211448)](/coal) [Coal](/coal) since it doesn't require fighting any monsters on the way and is relatively close to [Dorpat](/dorpat) town chest or Outpost chest.
