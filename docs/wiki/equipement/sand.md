Sand is a crafting material that is obtain by interacting with a Sand Pit while having a [Spade](/spade) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped. A player with level 1 [Mining](/mining) has a 35% success rate of obtaining Sand; the success rate can be increased to 100% with an increased Mining level (capping out at level 66 Mining). Successfully obtaining Sand grants 8 Mining [experience](/experience). Sand is commonly gathered and sold on the market by lower leveled players as a good source of income.

## Sand Pits

[![Sand](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/29/Sand.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143658)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/29/Sand.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141003143658)

Sand pits can also yield uncut crystals in various grades ranging from Low to Superior. They are located in several desert areas: [Dorpat](/dorpat), [Reval](/reval), [Moche I](/moche-i), and [Moche II](/moche-ii). Reval has the closest sand pit to a chest, so it is the most commonly used location.

## Uses

### Alchemy

Sand can be used to create vials using the [Alchemy](/alchemy) skill at a [Furnace](/furnace). Available vials are rolled from largest to smallest unless a player has a specific blueprint equipped. The vial that corresponds to the equipped blueprint will be given priority over the other sizes. All vials require 2 sand to smelt.
