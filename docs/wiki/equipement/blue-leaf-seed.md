**Blue Leaf Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 45 [Farming](/farming) and produces a [Blue Leaf](/blue-leaf) after 14 minutes. Harvesting the plant grants 45 Farming [experience](/experience).
