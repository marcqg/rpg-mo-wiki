## Craftable

Combine [Empty Large Vial](/empty-large-vial)[![Empty Large Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/af/Empty_Large_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155259)](/empty-large-vial) with water at a town [Water Well](/water-well) [![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)50 [Alchemy](/alchemy) - 2 exp.

## Can be Obtained From

Reclaim [Empty Large Vial](/empty-large-vial)[![Empty Large Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/a/af/Empty_Large_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225155259)](/empty-large-vial) after drinking most large potions.

## Uses

Equip it with an ingredient in your inventory to craft Large sized potions. Requires more than one of a particular ingredient to make a potion and it can fail giving no exp for the attempt. See [Alchemy](/alchemy) for more.

Also used in making [Soil](/soil) for your [Island](/island-deed).
