**Silver Emerald Necklace** is a necklace that gives a slight boost to [Magic](/magic). It can be crafted by using an Emerald and a [Silver Necklace](/silver-necklace) on a [Furnace](/furnace). A player with level 13 [Jewelry](/jewelry) has a 50% chance of successfully crafting the necklace; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 100% with an increased Jewelry level. Successfully crafting the necklace grants 67 Jewelry [experience](/experience).
