**Evil Green Santa Costume Box** is a [2021 Christmas event](https://forums.mo.ee/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=11254) item. It can be made by combining [Evil Green Santa Shirt](/evil-green-santa-shirt), [Evil Green Santa Pants](/evil-green-santa-pants), [Evil Green Santa Hat](/evil-green-santa-hat), [Evil Green Santa Gloves](/evil-green-santa-gloves), and [Evil Green Santa Boots](/evil-green-santa-boots) at an [Anvil](/anvil) while having a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) equipped.
