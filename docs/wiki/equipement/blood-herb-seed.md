**Blood Herb Seed** is a seed that can be planted in raked [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island) with level 67 [Farming](/farming) and produces a Blood Herb after 24 minutes. Harvesting the plant grants 100 Farming [experience](/experience). These seeds can be purchased from the [Wandering Farmer](/wandering-farmer) in [Whiland](/whiland) or obtained while cutting [Magic Oak Logs](/magic-oak-log). The chance of getting a seed while woodcutting is 1.5% and grants 55 [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) experience.
