**Feathers** are obtained through the [Alchemy](/alchemy) skill or from mob drops and are used in the [Fletching](/fletching) skill to make arrows. They can be bundled at an [Anvil](/anvil) using a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool).

## Alchemy

Feathers can be made using the Alchemy skill at level 20 by using a [Feather Cotton](/feather-cotton) and a flint at an [Alchemy Table](/alchemy-table). The success rate of making a feather depends on the quality of the flint used. Successfully creating a feather grants 20 Alchemy [experience](/experience).

*   Redhodium Flint - 100% success rate
*   Platinum Flint - 85% success rate
*   White Gold Flint - 60% success rate
*   Silver Flint - 60% success rate
*   Gold Flint - 50% success rate
