1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw Fish**Raw Blue Marine Fish** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Net](/fishing-net), and can be cooked into a [Blue Marine Fish](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Marine_Fish). The Raw Blue Marine Fish cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Ridder](/ridder) (4%)
*   [Crusader](/crusader) (4%)
