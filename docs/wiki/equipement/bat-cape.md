**Bat Cape** is a level 1 [Archery](/archery) [cape](/capes). It can be obtained as a drop from [Cave Bats](/cave-bat) in [Dungeon I](/dungeon-i). It can be enchanted into an Enchanted Bat Cape. Like most capes, having a Bat Cape equipped will allow the player into [Heaven](/heaven) and [Hell](/hell).
