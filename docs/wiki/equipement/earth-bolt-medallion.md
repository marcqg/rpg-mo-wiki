An Earth Bolt Medallion [![Earth Bolt Medallion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/98/Earth_Bolt_Medallion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225235012)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/98/Earth_Bolt_Medallion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225235012) can be used in [Spell Crafting](/spell-crafting) to make [Earth Bolt](/earth-bolt), and is required to make a Poison Rain Medallion.

## Crafting

### Earth Bolt

An Earth Bolt Medallion is used to make [Earth Bolt](/earth-bolt) from [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric) at the Earth Altar, requiring level 15 [Magic](/magic).

### Poison Rain Medallion

A Poison Rain Medallion can be made at an [anvil](/anvil) at level 80 [Forging](/forging). It has a 100% success rate but grants no [Experience](/experience).

Materials:

*   Earth Bolt Medallion
*   Earth Missile Medallion
*   Earth Wave Medallion
*   Earth Blast Medallion
