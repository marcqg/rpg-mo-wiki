A Cut Ruby [![Cut Ruby](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/57/Cut_Ruby.png/revision/latest?cb=20221218042720)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/57/Cut_Ruby.png/revision/latest?cb=20221218042720) is used in [Jewelry](/jewelry) to add aim and power to rings and necklaces and to make Gloves Of Power from ordinary gloves with [Forging](/forging), among other things.

## Crafting

### Cutting

A Cut Ruby can be crafted with level 14 [Jewelry](/jewelry) by using a [Chisel](/chisel) on an Uncut Ruby.

### 7 Souls Jewel

The [7 Souls Jewel](/7-souls-jewel) can be carfted at an [Anvil](/anvil) with level 99 [Forging](/forging) with 2 [Cut Diamonds](/cut-diamond), 2 Cut Rubies, 2 [Cut Emeralds](/cut-emerald), 2 [Cut Sapphires](/cut-sapphire), 1 Cut Amethyst, 1 Cut Black Opal, and 1 Cut Jadeite.

### Gloves Of Power

Gloves Of Power are crafted with [Forging](/forging) from 1 Cut Ruby, 1 Magic Rune and the base gloves. They each give 100 [Experience](/experience) if successful. The higher level Magic Rune used, the higher the success rate.
