**Necklace Mould** is a clay mould for necklaces used in the [Jewelry](/jewelry) skill with a value of 70 coins. It is made using one [clay](/clay) and one [tin](/tin) on a forge, and has a base 50% chance of success, which will go up to 100% at level 55 jewelry. It can be made at level 5 of the Jewelry skill and grants 10 experience for a successful attempt.

### Mob Drops

The only [monster](/monsters) that drops it is the [Frozen Bat](/frozen-bat) [![Frozen Bat](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Frozen_Bat.png/revision/latest?cb=20150401184614)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/87/Frozen_Bat.png/revision/latest?cb=20150401184614) at 0.46%.

### NPCs

They can be sold at [Reval Jeweler](/reval-jeweler), [Dorpat Jeweler](/dorpat-jeweler), andNarwa Jeweler.

The necklace mould is used with metal bars (iron, silver, gold, white gold, platinum, and redhodium) to make necklaces at the [Furnace](/furnace). Silver and higher metals can be set with cut gems for extra bonuses.
