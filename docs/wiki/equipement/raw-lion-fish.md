**Raw Lion Fish** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), and can be cooked into a [Lion Fish](/lion-fish). The Raw Lion Fish cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Rakblood Fishing Master](/rakblood-fishing-master)

### Drops

*   [Knight](/knight) (2%)
*   [Mummy](/mummy) (2%)
*   [Rotting Mummy](/rotting-mummy) (2%)
*   [Skeleton Mage](/skeleton-mage) (0.5%)
