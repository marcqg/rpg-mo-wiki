**Crystal Void Ice Arrow** is a level 90 [Archery](/archery) arrow that has a cooldown of 1.5 seconds, 8 speed, 7 range, and a max damage of 15. Each arrow has 25 uses. It can be crafted by a player with level 102 [Fletching](/fletching) at a [Fletching Table](/fletching-table) using a [Cut Crystal](/cut-crystal), [Void Log](/void-log), and Ice Feather. Crafting the arrow grants 72 Fletching [experience](/experience).
