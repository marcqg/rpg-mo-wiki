**Raw Pike** is a fish that can be caught by fishing with the [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod), and can be cooked into a [Pike](/pike). The Raw Pike cannot be consumed.

## Can be Obtained From

*   [Fishing](/fishing)
*   [Dorpat Fishing Master](/dorpat-fishing-master)
*   Fishing Off Dorpat Coast

### Drops

*   [Lion](/lion) (3%)
