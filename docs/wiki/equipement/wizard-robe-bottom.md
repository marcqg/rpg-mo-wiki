## Crafting

Wizard Robe Bottoms can be crafted with level 5 [Magic](/magic) using a Needle For Robe Bottoms (not consumed), 6 units of [Cotton Fabric](/cotton-fabric), and 2 units of [Thread](/thread), giving 30 [Experience](/experience). At any level, the chance of successfully crafting is 50%.
