**Iron Dagger** is a level 20 [Accuracy](/accuracy) weapon. It can be crafted by a player with level 20 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 2 [Iron Bars](/iron-bar) and an [Oak Log](/oak-log). The chance of successfully crafting one is 85% and rewards 48 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Iron Dagger can be enchanted into an Enchanted Iron Dagger.
