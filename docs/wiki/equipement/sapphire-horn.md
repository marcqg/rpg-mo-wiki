A Sapphire Horn [![Sapphire Horn](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f2/Sapphire_Horn.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202031501)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f2/Sapphire_Horn.png/revision/latest?cb=20230202031501) is a [Monster](/monsters) drop that can be used with [Forging](/forging), [Fletching](/fletching), and [Alchemy](/alchemy).

### [Alchemy](/alchemy)

A Sapphire Horn can be ground with a [Mortar And Pestle](/mortar-and-pestle) into [Sapphire Dust](/sapphire-dust) at level 50 [Alchemy](/alchemy).
