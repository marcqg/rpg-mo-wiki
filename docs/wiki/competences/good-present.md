Available from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) for 50 MOS or from your Achievement Rewards for 25 points, this item [![Good Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d5/Good_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/d5/Good_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728) contains a random item with a value up to 80,000 coins. Equip it in your [Inventory](/inventory) to receive your gift.

Four Good Presents can be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to make a [![Great Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/6/62/Great_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233729) [Great Present](/great-present) that contains a random item with a value up to 170,000 coins.

Four [Great Presents](/great-present) can be combined at the [Anvil](/anvil) to make a [![Best Present](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/3/34/Best_Present.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225233728) [Best Present](/best-present) that contains a random item with a value up to 450,000 coins.

Crafting of presents is 100% successful.
