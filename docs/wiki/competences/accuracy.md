Accuracy is a [Skill](/skills) that increases your [Hit Chance](/hit-chance) and is a requirement for one handed [Weapons](/weapons). It can be acquired from equipment in the form of Aim stats.

*   Increase it by setting [Fighting Mode](/fighting-mode) to Accurate or Controlled and do melee damage to mobs.
*   2 Aim = 1 accuracy.

## Effect on Combat

Accuracy is compared to mob Defense. As your adjusted Acc increases your hit chance raises up to 100%. Accuracy has no effect beyond 100% hit chance.

*   Strength also has an effect to raise chance to hit as evidenced by doing Combat Analysis on mobs with the same ACC and different STR.
