The Baby Observer pet is dropped by [King Observer](/king-observer), or [Flame Observer](/flame-observer), which can evolve to an [Observer (Common)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Observer_(Common)) pet (100,000 XP), then to a [King Observer (Common)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/King_Observer_(Common)) pet (500,000 XP), and finally to a [Demonic Observer (Rare)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Observer_(Rare)) pet (x2 Evolution Stones).
