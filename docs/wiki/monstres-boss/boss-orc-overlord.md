## Battle

When you locate the boss, right click and do a "Combat Analysis". It is recommended to have at least 60 health and a full inventory of food, so you'll probably be in the mid-60's for Character Level. As long as you can take the full damage presented by the Combat Analysis and heal between rounds, you should be set to do the 1200 damage needed to kill him.
