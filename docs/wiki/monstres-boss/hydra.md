The Hydra is an aggressive CL 62 NPC monster located in the [Dungeon I](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon).  
  
  

## Locations

| Location | Quantity | Coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dungeon I](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) | 8 | (80, 11) |
| [Dungeon I](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon) | 5 | (47, 24) |

To enter this section of the Dungeon, find the ladder (Point D, on the World Map) that is southwest of the Dorpat Outpost. The Dorpat Outpost is in the southeastern region of [Dorpat](/dorpat).
