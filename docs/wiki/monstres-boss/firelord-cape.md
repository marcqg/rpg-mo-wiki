Template:Infobox Item NewThe Firelord Cape can be obtained from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) for 100 mos points. It has wiki value 10,000. The [enchanting](/enchanting) progression splits at Firelord Dragon Wings +9 into [Defense](/defense) based wings and [Strength](/strength) based wings.

### Crafting at +9

To upgrade Firelord Dragon Wings +9 to Firelord War Dragon Wings +10 [Strength](/strength) wings or Firelord Peace Dragon Wings +10 [Defense](/defense) wings, you need to craft it with 3 Marks of War or of Peace at the [anvil](/anvil) with [Forging](/forging)  level 80. This craft has 100% success rate. After that the Firelord Peace/War Dragon Wings can be enchanted as normal up to level 120.
