The Observer is an aggressive CL 74 NPC monster located in the [Clouds](/clouds).

## Quest

"Floating Eyes", is the twenty-fifth quest in the game. Requiring 200 Observer kills to complete, 1 out of 3 rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion; these will either be...

\- 3 MOS

\- 2,592 Exp

\- 8,523 Coins

Observers are directly next to [Archangel](/archangel) groups, therefore bringing food and equipment to do two quests in a row is advised.
