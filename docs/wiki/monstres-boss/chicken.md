## Quest

The [Hen](/hen) and the Chicken are both capable of fulfilling the needed kills for the **Poultry Slaughtering** [quest](/quests). The quest requires 20 kills to complete, and one out of three rewards will be chosen to be given at random upon completion:

*   1 MOS
*   20 Exp
*   65 Coins
