The Islands Deed is your permanent teleport ticket to your own, personal island paradise. Can only be used inside a town, [Dragon's Lair](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Lair), [Fishing Guild](/fishing-guild-permission) and at chests in [Dungeon III](/dungeon-iii) and [Void I](/void-i). Purchase an Island Deed at any of the three farmers, [Dorpat Farmer](/dorpat-farmer), [Wandering Farmer](/wandering-farmer) or [Narwa Farmer](/narwa-farmer). They are also your main source of seeds for planting your [Farm](/farming-guide). You can only own one island and any deed you use will take you there. Template:Infobox Item
