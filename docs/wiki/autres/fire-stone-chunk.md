## Use

At lvl 80 Forging you can combine 6x **Fire Stone Chunks** at a Furnace to make [Fire Stone Bar](/fire-stone-bar).

Can be sold to NPCs for coins.

Can be sold on the Market.

## Mining

Requires lvl 80 mining and pickaxe or multi-tool.

Mined from [Hell](/hell),[No Man's Land](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/No_Man%27s_Land),[Dragon's Lair](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Lair).
