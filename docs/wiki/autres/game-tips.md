Game Tips are informational snippets displayed in the chat at random or on-demand using the /tips [chat command](/chat-commands).

| # | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | In defensive fight mode, each point of melee damage you deal will give 2 xp to the defense skill and 1 xp to health. |
| 2 | In accurate fight mode, each point of melee damage you deal will give 2 xp to the accuracy skill and 1 xp to health. |
| 3 | In aggressive fight mode, each point of melee damage you deal will give 2 xp to the strength skill and 1 xp to health. |
| 4 | In cordial fight mode, each point of melee damage you deal will give 2 xp to the to health skill. |
| 5 | In controlled fight mode, combat xp is equally divided between strength, defense and accuracy. Health will always get 1 xp per damage dealt. |
| 6 | Ingame wiki mod is a precious resource to plan your adventure. Access it typing /wiki in the chat line. |
| 7 | You can turn tips off from the Game Options menu. |
| 8 | The Enhanced Map mod adds several details to the game map, including key resource spots, travel points and boss locations. |
| 9 | The Enhanced Market mod adds several helpers for player's market operations, including the ability to resubmit expired offers and compare your equip with the one that is for sale. |
| 10 | Your health slowly regenerates over time: you gain 1 health every minute. |
| 11 | The more health a mob has, the more time it takes to respawn. |
| 12 | Be ready to be hunted down and fight for your life if you go into No Man's Land, the RPG MO PvP area. |
| 13 | Don't sell raw fish you get, cook it! |
| 14 | Better boots can increase your movement speed. |
| 15 | Food heals you. You can get food by killing chickens or by fishing. You may have to cook the food. |
| 16 | The forums have a lot of information about this game. You can access them on [https://forums.mo.ee/](https://forums.mo.ee/) . |
| 17 | Are you lost? Click on the minimap to enlarge it or save entire map with /savemap |
| 18 | If you die, you will lose all the items you are carrying but the two most valuable. Beware! |
| 19 | Potion of Preservation is a special potion that must be equipped and it will be consumed on death, allowing you to save a total of 7 items from your inventory. |
| 20 | If you have a pet equipped and you die you keep the pet. If it is not equipped, but it's in your inventory you may lose it. |
| 21 | When you die you do not lose the items you placed in your Pet's inventory. |
| 22 | You can buy an island deed from the farmer in Dorpat. |
| 23 | To see your combat level, mouse over yourself. To see the level of monsters, position the mouse pointer over them. |
| 24 | Public chat is global, meaning everyone online can read it! |
| 25 | Players chat is white, moderators chat is green and developers/admins chat is orange. |
| 26 | To whisper, type /w "\[playersname\]" followed by the message. |
| 27 | Type /online to bring up a list of players currently online. |
| 28 | Type /played to see how much time has passed since your first login. |
| 29 | Type /penalty to view or spend your current penalty points. |
| 30 | Type /wiki to access the ingame wiki database. |
| 31 | Type /xp to check the duration of an ongoing x2 Experience event. |
| 32 | x2 Experience server events are triggered by players using special x2 pots from the MOS market. |
| 33 | Need help? Don't be afraid to ask! RPG MO is full of helpful players! Further help and guides can be found in the game forums. |
| 34 | Higher accuracy will allow you to equip better weapons. |
| 35 | Higher defense will allow you to equip better armor. |
| 36 | Higher health will allow you to equip better jewelry. |
| 37 | Do not ignore Captchas! If you fail or ignore them you will get -5 penalty points and you will go to jail. |
| 38 | If you end up in jail, only a Game Moderator or an Admin can decide to free you. |
| 39 | You can save up to 5 captcha points. Using Captcha points will add XP to a skill of your choice. |
| 40 | You can access the player's market through the chest. |
| 41 | Some pets can be purchased from the pet vendor in Dorpat. Better pets are usually available on the market |
| 42 | A pet can extend your inventory space. Pets also give stats boosts. |
| 43 | Cross-chatting is bad. If someone is speaking in a chat channel, make sure you answer in the same one. |
| 44 | The best way to make money is through gathering professions (especially mining). There will always be players looking for iron, sand or coal in the player's market! |
| 45 | Through the market you can place "buy" or "sell" offers, and you can check buy and sell offers from other players. |
| 46 | MOS is a special currency that can be acquired with real money. It allows you to buy special items from the MOS store. |
| 47 | The MOS Store is accessible from the "Buy items and coins" link at the bottom right of page. |
| 48 | To use magic you need to buy and equip a magic pouch, fill it with spell scrolls (all available at Dorpat Magician NPC) and engage in combat. |
| 49 | You are allowed to have a max of 5 accounts (alts), but trading items or materials between your own accounts is not allowed. |
| 50 | Please check RPG MO Code of Conduct on [https://mo.ee/rules](https://mo.ee/rules) |
| 51 | Please support us on Patreon [https://www.patreon.com/rpgmo](https://www.patreon.com/rpgmo) |
| 52 | Join our official Discord server [https://mo.ee/discord](https://mo.ee/discord) |
