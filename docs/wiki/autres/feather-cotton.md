**Feather Cottons** are obtained through the [Farming](/farming) skill and are used in the [Alchemy](/alchemy) skill to create [Feathers](/feather) and [Feather Bundles](/feather-bundle).

## Farming

At level 20 Farming, a [Cotton Tree Seed](/cotton-tree-seed) can be planted in [Soil](/soil) on a [Player Island](/player-island). After 13 minutes, it can be harvested into a Feather Cotton.
