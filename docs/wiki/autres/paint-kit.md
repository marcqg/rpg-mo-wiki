Template:Infobox Item NewThe Paint kit is a unique item in RPG-MO. It allows the player to dye items placed on a Armor Stand, pets placed in a Pet Shelter, and pets placed in a [Nest](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Nest). This is the only item in the game that allows items to be recolored. The only way to obtain one is from the player or MOS market.

Use by adding them to the specific slot in the armor stand, then right click the equipment slot you would like to recolor.
