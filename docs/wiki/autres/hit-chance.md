Hit chance is the chance of an attack to do physical damage. [Accuracy](/accuracy) and [Equipment](/equipment) (aim) can increase a players adjusted [Accuracy](/accuracy). [Defense](/defense) decreases your opponents chance to hit you. An opponent with higher levels of accuracy will have a higher hit chance and an opponent with higher defense will decrease your chance to hit them.

Every (1) point of [Accuracy](/accuracy) negates (1) point of [Mob](/monsters) [Defense](/defense).

*   For a 100% hit chance your [Accuracy](/accuracy) needs to be @ or above your opponents [Defense](/defense).
*   Higher Accuracy has no direct benefit.

The /[Mods](/mods) item 'Right-Click Menu Extensions' enables the option "Combat Analysis" which shows the precise hit chances for both the player and the [Mob](/monsters).

## Note

[Magic Damage](/magic) follows its own set of rules and spells ignore hit chance.

*   Spell Damage is added to your next melee hit.

*   Aim is for the Melee Attack.

Archery Damage follows its own set of rules also and needs no Accuracy skill points.
