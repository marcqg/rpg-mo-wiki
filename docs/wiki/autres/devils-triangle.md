Devil's Triangle is accessed via the Illusion Gate in the north-east corner of [Walco](/walco), and leads to [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest). This map is divided into small islands, connected through one-way illusion gates which makes it very easy to get lost.

### Paths

U means take the upper-left gate, D means take the lower-right gate.

*   [Walco](/walco) : DDDU, then talk to the Traitor NPC on the original island

*   [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest): DDDD

*   [Azurite](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Azurite) vein: UD, to Walco: UUU

*   Platinum vein: DUUUD\* or DUDDU, to Walco: DDU

*   Redhodium and [Dragon's Blood Tree](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon%27s_Blood_Tree): UUDDD or DDUDD or DUDDDU\*, to Walco: DU

*   Blizzard Altar: DDUD or UUDD, to Walco: DDU

*   Dragonstone: DDUDUD or UUDDUD or UDDUDD or DUDDDD\*, to Walco: UDUU

\*lowest cl required for path

## NPCs

Traitor at entrance in [Walco](/walco) says, "Somewhere near Skeletons you should find an Illusion gate. Beware it's full of traps." The Illusion Gate is located at (86, 85) and takes you to Devil's Triangle. Traitor located on an island in Devil's Triangle is the exit back to [Walco](/walco). Click him to return.
