Core of Knowledge is a place dedicated to spell crafting, giving way to six (flash, air, earth, water, fire and poison) magic altars. Core of Knowledge is accessed using a specific permission on one of the five portals present on the Star of Knowledge map. Each permission needs wizardry level to use.
