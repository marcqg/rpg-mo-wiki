Since the game launched there has been an Annual Halloween Event, with Halloween being the very first event of the game. Like all events, there are Halloween themed items, in the form of weapons, armor, food, island decor, and pets!

One may be wondering what is the purpose of this compared to the In-game wiki and collection book. Indeed, in many cases the Collection book is more useful, but it can hardly be considered convenient, as it either requires 20m or so to buy or to go to a friend's island who has the Book. Additionally, it requires you to be on your island. One particular thing this page has that the book does not, is how the items were originally acquired, which plays a large factor in determining the price of an item. (ie. pets acquired from gathering are far more valuable than those from the scavenger hunts)

The "Craftable" column is for if the item is still craftable today. Some items were crafted originally, but now the option to craft is no longer available.
