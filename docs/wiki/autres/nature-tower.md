Nature Tower is located in [Void I](/void-i) land. It is the easiest of the three towers, [Ice Tower](/ice-tower) being medium difficulty and [Fire Tower](/fire-tower) being the hardest. Completing each room gains you 400 experience points towards your [Fighting Mode](/fighting-mode) stance. If you use arrows to deal the final hit to the mob, [Archery](/archery) skill gets the bonus. There are 44 (Elite) mob rooms and [Gate](/gate) is in room 45. Like [Cathedral](/cathedral), all of your items are safe when you die and you exit outside of Nature Tower with full health.

The total amount of HP for all mobs excluding the [Gate](/gate) is 108,180. Without using [Magic](/magic) and assuming a [combat stance](/fighting-mode) other than [Cordial](/fighting-mode), finishing the Nature Tower will grant you a total of 350,940 combat experience with 233,960 for your current [combat stance](/fighting-mode) and 116,980 for [Health](/health). This includes the total bonus experience of 17,600 which comes from dealing the final hit to each mob. Using the [Cordial](/fighting-mode) combat stance will give 2/3 of the total experience normally gained with all of the experience going to [Health](/health). Using [Magic](/magic) will change the mob experience due to how [Magic](/magic) experience is calculated.

The bonus experience per room is 400.
