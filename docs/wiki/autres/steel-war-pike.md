**Steel War Pike** is a level 40 [Accuracy](/accuracy) weapon. It can be crafted by a player with level 42 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or a [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 3 [Steel Bars](/steel-bar) and 3 [Willow Logs](/willow-log). The chance of successfully crafting one is 70% and rewards 135 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Steel War Pike can be enchanted into an Enchanted Steel War Pike.
