Whiland is a forest zone with waypoints to [Dorpat](/dorpat) (sign at south-west corner), [Rakblood](/rakblood) (ladder in the center of the map), and the [Lost Woods](/lost-woods) (sign at north-west corner). Whiland, along with [Walco](/walco), is one of the two recommended zones to visit after Dorpat, with Whiland having a larger emphasis on non-combat skills. There are only 2 landmarks, the Main City and the [Earth Altar](/spell-crafting). Whiland is also the home of the [Wandering Farmer](/wandering-farmer).
