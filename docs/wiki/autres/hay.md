**Hay** is a level 5 [Farming](/farming) crop produced after planting [Hay Seeds](/hay-seed) and waiting 4 minutes.

## Alchemy

Hay is used as a secondary ingredient in [Archery](/archery) potions. The amount of Hay needed varies with the size of the potion.

## Breeding

Hay is used as fodder for following [Breeding](/breeding) pets. The total amount needed for the pet is listed in parentheses.

*   [Donkey (Common)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Donkey_(Common)) (3)
*   [Horror Steed (Rare)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Horror_Steed_(Rare)) (21)
*   White Horse (Rare) (14)
*   [Underworld Steed (Legendary)](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Steed_(Legendary)) (4)

## Carpentry

Hay is used as an ingredient in various [Carpentry](/carpentry) recipes.

*   Haypile: 10 Hay
*   [Breeding Nest](/breeding-nest): 300 [Fir Log](/fir-log) + 100 Hay + 100 [Wheat](/wheat)
