Nori Seaweed can be acquired by achieving level 70 [Fishing](/fishing) by using a [Fishing Net](/fishing-net) on a net fishing spot. It is used as an ingredient in various kinds of sushi in the [Cooking](/cooking) skill, and can also be used to craft the _Large Potion Of Mining_. Also drops from Fire Viper
