Necklaces are made using the [Jewelry](/jewelry) skill or purchased on the MOS Market using the premium currency [MOS](/mos). Players can equip Necklaces under their neck [Equipment](/equipment) slot for a stat boost dependent on the Necklace worn.

Necklaces provide stat boosts for every attribute except Speed. Purchasing a Necklace that will provide a great amount of increase for all of these stats can prove to become very expensive for players on a tight budget.

The [Dorpat Jeweler](/dorpat-jeweler) is the only Jeweler that currently has Necklaces for sale although there are Jewelers in [Heaven](/heaven), [Narwa](/narwa), [Rakblood](/rakblood) and [Reval](/reval) that allow you to sell an assortment of Necklaces to them for half their value.
