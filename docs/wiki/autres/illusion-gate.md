(Image not available) Illusion Gates are portals used to move from one land to another similar to Traveler's Signs. [Walco](/walco) has one gate which leads to [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle). Every island in [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle) has two gates which lead to other islands or [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest). [Broceliande Forest](/broceliande-forest) has one gate to return to the entrance island in [Devil's Triangle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Devil%27s_Triangle).

[Void I](/void-i) has two Illusion Gates. One leads to a gate in front of [Clouds](/clouds) town and one leads to a gate south east of the Fire Altar in [Blood River](/blood-river).
