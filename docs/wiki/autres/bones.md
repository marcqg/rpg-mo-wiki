Bones [![Bones](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/5e/Bones.png/revision/latest?cb=20230126011838)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/5e/Bones.png/revision/latest?cb=20230126011838) are used for [Breeding](/breeding) and [Forging](/forging), and can be crafted into [Dust](/dust) with [Alchemy](/alchemy).

## Forging

Items forged using Bones:

*   [Bone Shield](/bone-shield)
*   [Bone Long Sword](/bone-long-sword)
*   Bone Dagger

## Monster Drops

Monsters that drop Bones, with their chance of dropping them:

*   [Skeleton Knight](/skeleton-knight) (5%)
*   [Giant Troll](/giant-troll) (10%)
*   [Desert Runner](/desert-runner) (10%)
*   [Desert Orc](/desert-orc) (10%)
*   [Bear](/bear) (10%)
