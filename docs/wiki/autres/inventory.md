Your Inventory contains all of the items you pick up during your travels throughout the world of RPG MO. Collectable items (Loot Drops) from [Mobs](/monsters) or resources are automatically put into your Inventory, if there is room. [Mining](/mining), [Woodcutting](/woodcutting) or Fishing will continue to fill your Inventory until full unless you move your character away from the resource.

It is also the interface between the [Chest](/chest), [Anvil](/anvil) [![Anvil](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/d/dd/Anvil.png/revision/latest?cb=20180808222015), [Furnace](/furnace) [![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050), [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611), [Kettle](/cooking) [![Kettle](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/5b/Kettle.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075842)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/5/5b/Kettle.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075842)

and [Water Well](/water-well). [![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)

Having the in-game Wiki Mod enabled allows you to right-click the [Furnace](/furnace), [Campfire](/cooking), [Kettle](/cooking), [Water Well](/water-well), Fishing Spots, Mining locations, Trees, and Mob's to check 'Drops' to see your actual success percentage and experience gains. Right-click [NPC](/npcs) 'Drops' to see a list of items they sell with quantities. Info is displayed in the chat window.

## Equipment

All [Tools](/tools), [Armor](/armors) and [Weapons](/weapons) can be equipped by left clicking on them in your Inventory. A yellow border around the item indicates that it is equipped. A small, green dot appears in the upper left corner of the two most valuable items in your Inventory. These items will be saved upon [Death](/death). If you have a [Potion of Preservation](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_of_Preservation) equipped, five additional items will have the green dot and will be saved upon [Death](/death). Enabling the 'Gear Screen [Mod](/mods)' allows you to open a mini-inventory window showing your equipped items, your character icon and Bonus stats. This is useful to ensure that all of the equipment you want is being worn by your character.

With a [Magic Pouch](/magic-pouches) equipped, selecting a magic spell will put that spell into the pouch, if there is room for it. Bigger pouches can carry more spells and may have other bonuses.

Using the 'Keybinding' [Mod](/mods), you can move items between your [Chest](/chest), Inventory and [Pet Inventory](/pets) with a single keystroke. With the proper [Mods](/mods) enabled, you can 'ctrl' click an inventory item and a green box will momentarily appear around the item which will 'lock' those items in your Inventory when you move them using the Keybinding [Mod](/mods). 'Ctrl' click the item again and a red box will momentarily appear around the item turning off the item lock.

## Consumables

Equipping (left clicking on) cooked [food](/foods), [Potions](/alchemy) or [Teleport Scrolls](/teleport-scrolls) causes them to be consumed whether you need them or not. Right clicking allows you to "Use" (equip/eat), "Inspect" (see info in chat window) or "Destroy" an item without using it (I'm collecting ore and don't want the [Monster](/monsters) drops filling my inventory slots). Enabling the 'Right-Click Menu Extensions' [Mod](/mods), provides more information options.

## Creation

[Alchemy](/alchemy) is performed in your Inventory. Select the ingredients you want to use to attempt to create a potion.

The [Mortar and Pestle](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Mortar_and_Pestle) [![Mortar And Pestle](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f0/Mortar_And_Pestle.png/revision/latest?cb=20150314215156)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/f/f0/Mortar_And_Pestle.png/revision/latest?cb=20150314215156) are used in the Inventory to make powders (Dust) for [Alchemy](/alchemy) potions.

Chipped Gem processing is also performed in your Inventory. Select the [Chisel](/chisel) [![Chisel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Chisel.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215614)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/2/2b/Chisel.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225215614)and the chipped gem to attempt to make a cut Gem. Gems are used to make [Jewelry](/jewelry).

[Rings](/jewelry) and [Necklaces](/jewelry) are made by [Forging](/forging). Equip the appropriate mold and ingredients then click on the [Furnace](/furnace).[![Furnace](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/9/9f/Furnace.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135050)You can also equip plain jewelry and cut gems then click the furnace to attempt to forge jewelry with gems like a Silver Sapphire Ring.

Eating [Cooked](/cooking) fish is a great way to refill your [Health](/health) bar after battles. Select the raw fish then click the [Campfire](/cooking) [![Campfire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/0/01/Campfire.png/revision/latest?cb=20140210075611) to attempt to cook it. Mmmm, Mmmm good. Raw or cooked fish is also a good source of coins by selling them to the various [Fishing Masters](/list-of-npcs).

Selecting different ingredients and then selecting the [Kettle](/cooking) can result in higher health regenerating meals than just [Campfire Cooking](/cooking). See [Cooking](/cooking) for recipe combinations.

Fill empty vials [![Empty Small Vial](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/81/Empty_Small_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225162405)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/8/81/Empty_Small_Vial.png/revision/latest?cb=20150225162405) at the [Well](/water-well) by equipping the vial and then clicking the [Well](/water-well) [![Water Well](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/c/ce/Water_Well.gif/revision/latest?cb=20141004135601) with 100% success.

## Extra Space

If you have a [Pet](/pets), it has Inventory carrying capacity depending on the pet with 4, 8, 12 or 16 extra slots. Put your [Pet](/pets) into your Inventory and Equip it. A [Pet](/pets) Icon (yellow dragon) will appear to the right of your Skills Icon. Hover over it and select "Open pet menu". This opens a new inventory window. Single click an item in either side to move it to the other side. See[Mods](/mods) for more Pet inventory options.

## The Chest

Clicking on the [Chest](/chest) in a town or on your [Island](/island-deed), opens the [Chest](/chest) Inventory. Use the [Chest](/chest) commands located at the bottom of the [Chest](/chest) window to move items between your [Chest](/chest) and your Inventory. Enabling the 'Chest interface' [Mod](/mods) will give you more movement and sorting options.
