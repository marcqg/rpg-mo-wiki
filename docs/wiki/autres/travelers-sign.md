Traveler Signs can be located at various places on each map and are used to teleport to another connected map in the world of RPG MO.  Connections can be viewed using the World Map by first selecting the Mini-Map located in the upper left corner of the playing field.  Enabling the most recent [Mods](/mods) will provide more information

With the Enhanced Map mod loaded, the Travelers Sign is indicated by a light blue circle on the mini-map.  Hovering over each circle provides the destination information for that Travelers Sign.
