**Bronze Helmet** is a level 1 [Defense](/defense) helmet. It can be crafted by a player with level 6 [Forging](/forging) at an [Anvil](/anvil) with a [Forging Hammer](/forging-hammer) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool) using 4 [Bronze Bars](/bronze-bar). The chance of successfully crafting one is 100% and rewards 40 Forging [experience](/experience).

A Bronze Helmet can be enchanted into an Enchanted Bronze Helm.
