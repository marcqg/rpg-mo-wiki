1.  REDIRECT Template:Raw FishA **Raw Rock Squid** is a type of fish that can be caught at [Fishing](/fishing) level 65 with a [Fishing Rod](/fishing-rod) giving 80 fishing experience. It can be cooked into a [Rock Squid](/rock-squid) at [Cooking](/cooking) level 65 giving 156 cooking experience. When cooked and eaten, Rock Squid heals 12 [Health](/health).
