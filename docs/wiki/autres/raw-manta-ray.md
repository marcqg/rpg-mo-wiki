**Raw Manta Ray** is a fish that is obtained by fishing with a [Wooden Harpoon](/wooden-harpoon) or [Multi-tool](/multi-tool). A player with 58 [Fishing](/fishing) has a 35% chance of successfully catching the fish; the success rate can be increased to a maximum of 45% with an increased Fishing level. Successfully catching this fish will grant 70 Fishing [experience](/experience).

A [Cooking](/cooking) level of 58 is required to cook into a [Cooked Manta Ray](/cooked-manta-ray) at a [Campfire](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire).
