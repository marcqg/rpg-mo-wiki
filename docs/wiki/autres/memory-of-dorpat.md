Memory of Dorpat is created by combining 1 [Small Vial of Water](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Small_Vial_of_Water) with 6 [Dorpat Souls](/dorpat-soul). Consuming Memory of Dorpat will teleport the player to coordinates (20,20) in [Dorpat](/dorpat). Crafting the potion requires level 24 [Alchemy](/alchemy), has a 50% base chance of success, an 80% maximum chance of success, and will yield 35 [Alchemy](/alchemy) experience.
