_Moche_ I is located directly north-west of [Reval](/reval) and contains monsters of various levels, as well as locations for [Mining](/mining) and [Sand](/sand) collection. The City of Moche I, being in the center of the map includes 2 [Chests](/chest), a [Furnace](/furnace), a [Water Well](/water-well) and 4 NPCs. The Woodcutter's guild can also be located on the small island in the south.

## **Entrances**

*   By using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in north-west [Dorpat](/dorpat) (8,60), you'll arrive in south-west Moche I. (94,42)
*   By using the [Travelers Sign](/travelers-sign) in the south-east of [Reval](/reval) (47,8), you'll arrive in North-west Moche I. (46,91)
