[![2x Experience Potion](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/46/2x_Experience_Potion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226014631)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/rpg-mo/images/4/46/2x_Experience_Potion.png/revision/latest?cb=20150226014631) The 2x Experience Potion can be purchased from the [MOS Market](/mos-market) for 500 MOS or 10 for 4200 MOS and has a wiki value of 449,000 coins. It is also the drop item from any of the [Gate](/gate) mobs. Some players also purchase these potions and sell them to other players in the player [Market](/market-guide). The player drinking (equipping) a 2x potion begins a 30 minute 2x event for all players in all worlds causing any experience points gained to be doubled including [Captcha](/captcha), [Daily Rewards](/daily-rewards) Exp, [Party Dungeon](https://rpg-mo.fandom.com/wiki/Party_Dungeons) Rewards Exp and [Kill Quest](/quests) Rewards Exp. Using more than one potion increases the 2x event timer by 30 minutes each. The following are the personal bonuses given by using a 2x potion - NOTE: These bonuses do not stack.

+15% Accuracy  
+15% Strength  
+15% Defense  
+15% Magic  
+15% Woodcutting  
+15% Fishing  
+15% Cooking  
+15% Mining  
+15% Archery
